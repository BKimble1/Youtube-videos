# ElevenLabs voice auditions (backup)

`ElevenLabs_voice_auditions.zip` holds the 12 audition takes (3 voices × 4 takes, Eleven v4),
`eval.json`, `scribe_blind.json`, `AUDITION_REPORT.md` and the connector pipeline test clips.

Rebuild with `sh reconstruct.sh` (Linux/macOS) or `python3 reconstruct.py` (any OS); both verify
SHA-256 for every part and the whole file.
