## What the audience learns, sequence by sequence

Times are estimates at ~170 wpm with holds.

1. **Cold open (0:00–0:15).** One man's imagined movement fired a cannon with another man's finger, and the finger's
   owner couldn't even see the game.
2. **The split game (0:15–0:47).** In 2013 at UW, Rao could see the city and tell planes from rockets, but only Stocco,
   across campus, had the fire key.
3. **The route (0:47–1:42).** The signal's full path:
   - imagined right-hand movement;
   - an EEG rhythm change;
   - the cursor climbs to Fire;
   - one internet command;
   - a pulse over the left motor cortex;
   - the right hand jerks up and comes down on the space bar.
   It felt like a nervous tic, and the first session was lost to network trouble.
4. **The control (1:42–2:16).** In 2014, flipping the coil kept the same game and the same pulse strength but removed
   the effect. With the coil in place: 83 % and 25 % hits. Flipped: zero presses. So the pulse, not guessing, made the
   clicks.
5. **BrainNet (2:16–3:00).** Three brains played a falling-block game. Two Senders who could see the gap voted with EEG.
   The Receiver read each vote as a flash, or no flash, from a coil over the visual cortex, over two rounds.
6. **Trust and payoff (3:00–3:32).** Software secretly corrupted one Sender's votes on 10 of 16 trials. Receivers
   learned whom to trust and cleared the line about 13/16 times. The pulse went from moving a hand to giving advice
   you can overrule.

## Proposed corrections (Blake's call)

Full evidence for each is in `SOURCE_NOTES.md` (C11, C22, C18).

1. **Paragraph 6: the "lab notes" and the failed connection.**
   - **Source:** the public pilot write-up says "Session 1 was terminated early due to network communication issues,
     which were resolved as the subjects waited for the next session."
   - **Problem:** that isn't lab notes, and it doesn't say the connection failed.
   - **Current:** "There's a detail I love in the lab notes. Their first session ended early because the network
     connection failed."
   - **Proposed:** "There's a detail I love in their pilot report. Their first session ended early because of network
     problems."
   - "Two human brains, ready to go. Bad connection." stays.
2. **Paragraph 11: the second chance.**
   - **Problem:** after the first choice, the Senders see the half-fallen block and send new votes. The Receiver's
     second chance comes with fresh advice, and the visuals will have to show it.
   - **Current:** "…chose what to do, then had a second chance to change their answer."
   - **Proposed:** "…chose what to do, then got a fresh round of votes and a chance to change their answer."
3. **Paragraph 10: the year.**
   - **Problem:** the BrainNet paper is from 2019, but its preprint went up in September 2018, so the games were
     played in 2018 or earlier.
   - **Current:** "In 2019, three people played…"
   - **Proposed:** "In a 2019 study, three people played…"
   - "Six years later" in the ending can stay: 2013 to the 2019 paper.
