**Title:** This Robot Is Wearing Living Human Skin

**Description:**

Real cultured human tissue, anchored to a machine and pulled into a smile. In 2024, University of Tokyo researchers
held living skin to a robotic face with tiny ligament-inspired anchors. In an earlier 2022 study, they cut the living
skin on a robotic finger, grafted a collagen sheet, and after seven days in culture the tissue had sealed the wound.

The machine isn't alive. The skin is.
