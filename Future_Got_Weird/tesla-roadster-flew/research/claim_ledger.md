## Differences from the brief

1. **0.81 s.** The brief is right that it was a Tesla test. The page's own targets are lower (under 0.59 s for Rocket, C9).
   The Short uses only 0.81, labeled as Tesla's own test.
2. **"Five of its own cars."** Some of the five vehicles are trucks (Semi, Cybertruck), so the script says "vehicles".
3. **"The stunt version is an invite-only, single-seat Rocket."** No source confirms that the demo car *was* a Rocket
   trim. It is safer to say that Tesla's page lists flight on the Rocket only. The Short says "the version that flies
   is the Rocket".
4. **Inverted hang and launch.** According to Engadget these were separate runs, so the edit must not imply one
   continuous take. The Short labels them as excerpts. The companion says "on a second run".
5. **Reuters, Road & Track, Business Insider and The Verge** (the brief's sources) could not be opened here. Search
   didn't surface the BI or Verge pieces. Their claims are covered by the other outlets above.
