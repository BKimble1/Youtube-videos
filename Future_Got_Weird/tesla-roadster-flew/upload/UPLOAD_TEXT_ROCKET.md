## Related Video (the clickable hand-off)

The spoken call to action is "It also drives upside down. Tap the linked breakdown.", with one text cue, "How it drives
upside down". The link itself has to be set in Studio; a URL pasted in a Short's description or comment is not
clickable.

1. Upload the Short and let it finish processing.
2. In YouTube Studio, open the Short (Content → Shorts → the Short's details).
3. Under **Related video**, choose the existing video **"Tesla's Flying Roadster Isn't What You Think"** (video ID
   `ujV0J6-cfX8`, viewer URL https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujV0J6-cfX8). Use the viewer video, not a Studio editing
   link.
4. Save. Open the published Short on a phone and confirm the related-video link appears under the channel handle and
   opens `ujV0J6-cfX8`. Only then is the hand-off done.

Requirements to confirm in the account before relying on it (from the brief; YouTube Help could not be opened from this
container, see below):

- the channel needs **advanced features** access for Related Video;
- the target video must be **public or unlisted**.
