## Related Video (the clickable hand-off)

The spoken call to action is "Full Roadster breakdown in the linked video." The link itself has to be set in Studio; a URL
pasted in a Short's description or comment is not clickable.

1. Upload the Short and let it finish processing.
2. In YouTube Studio, open the Short (Content → Shorts → the Short's details).
3. Under **Related video**, choose the existing video **"Tesla's Flying Roadster Isn't What You Think"**
   (video ID `ujV0J6-cfX8`, viewer URL https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujV0J6-cfX8). Use the viewer video, not a Studio
   editing link.
4. Save. Viewers then see a clickable related-video link under the channel handle on the Short.

Requirements to confirm in the account before relying on it (from the brief; YouTube Help could not be opened from this
container, see below):

- the channel needs **advanced features** access for Related Video;
- the target video must be **public or unlisted**.

**Not verified here, so not claimed:** that the account has advanced features, that `ujV0J6-cfX8` is public, and that its
title is exactly as above. youtube.com and support.google.com are blocked by this session's network policy (proxy 403).
A search-result summary of YouTube Help ("Add a related video", answer 14075157) agrees with the steps above and says the
link appears "below your channel handle"; another (answer 13748639) says Shorts description/comment URLs are
non-clickable while the Related Video link is clickable. The advanced-features and public/unlisted requirements were not
confirmed by that summary. Check them in Studio.

**Don't use:** "breaks the laws of physics", "illegal", "will explode", any pressure, mass or duration figures, an
end-screen promise, or a drawn "button" (there is none in the render; the CTA is a plain text cue).

**Before upload, recheck** Tesla's live Roadster page (claim ledger, "Rocket Short recheck"): the trim names, that flight
is listed only for the Rocket version, and that the thrusters use compressed air. The opening label and the line "These
jets blast compressed air" depend on them.
