## Changes from v3

1. **Opening:** v3 held the hovering car for 0.53 s, then cut to the overhead view. v4 opens on 1.55 s of continuous
   lift-off, with the ground and the parked cars below to show height. The words are "Tesla just made a car fly." and
   then "And hang upside down." on the car under the ramp. The repeated "hung upside down… On another run, it flew off,
   rolled upright, and landed" is gone. The roll is covered by the airborne explainer, the landing by the close.
2. **Suction:** 7.8 s cut to 6.2 s, with the owner's wording. There are three beats, each labelled as spoken ("FANS
   PULL AIR OUT", "LOW PRESSURE", "SURROUNDING AIR" then "PRESSES IT TO THE RAMP"). The "THIN GAP" beat and the camera
   drift are removed. The arrows keep the same direction throughout (outside air pushes the upside-down car up into the
   ramp).
3. **0–60:** v3 counted 0.81 up over the live run, with "TESLA'S CLAIM · ITS OWN TEST" and a two-part note (three text
   boxes). v4 has one block, "Tesla's claimed best / 0.81 s", and one plate, "Shown run: 1.00 s". The footage is now the
   run that label describes: the stream's timed clip, whose own timer stops at 01.00 (`rec_225218`). Nothing animates
   0.81 in sync with that run. Length: 4.2 s (v3: 4.05 s).
4. **Ending:** "So the demo flew. A road-ready flying car? Not yet." became "It flew. Road-ready? Not yet." The road-use
   caveat is said once, in the Rocket line. The close is a bright upright airborne shot instead of the dark underside
   view. The loop now resolves naturally rather than with a forced seam: the landing descent at the end, the lift-off
   at the start.
5. **Length:** 36.7 s became 31.1 s. The requested narration is shorter than v3's. The pace stays about 180 wpm and
   wasn't padded to reach 33 s.
6. **Mix:** the real event sound is ducked under every word and swells only in the gaps (the Semi touchdown after
   "included.", the launch blast between "60?" and "0.81"). Every word clears the effects and event sound by at least
   12 dB (median 31 dB).
