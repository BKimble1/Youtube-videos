## Transition list

Each entry gives the outgoing subject → incoming subject → new information → decision.

1. The living frog → the heart → the heart stops → **keep** (one push).
2. The heart → the trunk → ice through the body → **keep** (the window widens).
3. The trunk → the cells (wide) → ice between cells → **keep** (a dive).
4. Wide → closer → water leaves, cells shrink → **keep** (one glide; a new step).
5. Closer → one cell → the spear's damage → **keep** (the same glide).
6. The cell → the liver → the source of the sugar → **keep**.
7. The liver and vein → the capillary → the sugar in the blood → **keep** (the camera follows the glow).
8. The capillary → the cell → the protection → **keep**.
9. The cell → winter → survival → **keep** (one pull-out).
10. Winter → the sky → time passing → **keep** (the camera rises).
11. Spring → the heart → the restart → **keep**.
12. The heart → the trunk → circulation → **keep**.
13. The trunk → the whole frog → it moves → **keep**.
14. Hop 1 → hop 2 → frame 0's composition → **keep**.

No cuts: every change of subject is a camera move through the same body or the same forest.
