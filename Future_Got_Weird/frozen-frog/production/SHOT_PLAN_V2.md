## Transition list

Each entry gives the outgoing subject → incoming subject → new information → decision.

1. The living frog → the heart through the chest → the heart stops as the wave freezes the frog → **keep** (one push).
2. The frozen heart → the trunk cross-section → ice spreading through the body → **keep** (the window widens).
3. The trunk → the cells → the danger: ice between cells, a spear denting one → **keep** (a dive).
4. The threatened cell → the trunk, framed on the liver → the source of the sugar → **keep**.
5. The liver and vein → inside a capillary → glucose travelling in the blood → **keep** (the camera follows the glow).
6. The capillary → the threatened cell in close-up → the protection → **keep** (a pan; the same cell, a new
   composition).
7. The cell → the frozen frog under snow → survival over weeks → **keep** (one pull-out).
8. Winter → the sky → time passing → **keep** (the camera rises; it is not a cut).
9. Spring → the heart → the restart → **keep**.
10. The heart → the trunk → circulation returning → **keep** (the window widens again).
11. The trunk → the face → the eyes open, the legs move → **keep**.
12. The face → the leap → the landing in frame 0's composition → **keep**.

## The loop

The frog lands in the opening's spot, and the camera ends on frame 0's framing. The opening's living motion (the head
rising, the step, the blink) runs on the loop clock, so it starts in the last second and continues through frame 0. The
heartbeat period (4.9/11 s) puts beats at 1.336 s (the final one), 19.600 s (the restart), 24.055 s and on frame 0.
