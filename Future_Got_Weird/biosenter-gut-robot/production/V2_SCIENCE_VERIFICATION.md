## Narration check

No correction is needed. The narration is defensible as written:

- **"Tiny spikes that pop out of its body"**: the coverage describes tiny needle-like features emerging as the sleeves
  expand.
- **"Almost like a snake"**: the device is described as snake-inspired.
- **"Over SIX FEET"**: about 2 m is 6.5 ft.
- **"Take tissue samples and deliver liquid"**: biopsy clips, and a fluid sprayer for drug delivery.

## Flags (no change made; decide before publishing)

1. **Sleeve colour.** The sleeves are described as polyimide, which is usually amber (Kapton-like). V2 keeps the teal
   look, as the brief requires. It is a stylization, not a factual claim.
2. **The tag "PIG STUDY · MIT 2026".** The study was MIT-led, with UT Southwestern co-authors (B. Ying). The tag is
   accurate as it stands. "PIG STUDY · 2026" would avoid naming only one institution.
3. **Speed.** The pig diagram is a time-lapse; the 11 minutes is not shown, and nothing claims a speed.
4. **Before publishing**, confirm against the paper itself: the exact chamber layout, the scale geometry, and that
   biopsy and spray delivery were demonstrated in the pig studies specifically, not only on the bench.
