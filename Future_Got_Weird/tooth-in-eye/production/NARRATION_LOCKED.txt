Doctors put a TOOTH inside this man’s EYE… and he could SEE AGAIN. His cornea was so damaged, a normal transplant wouldn’t work. So surgeons removed one of his teeth with a piece of bone, drilled a hole through it, and fitted a clear lens inside. They tucked it beneath the skin near his other eye for months, letting living tissue grow around it. Then they implanted it into his damaged eye, allowing light to reach his retina. After TEN YEARS blind… he could see again.
