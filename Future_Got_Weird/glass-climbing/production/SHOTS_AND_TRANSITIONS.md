# Glass Climbing V1: shots, transitions and what moves on every arrival

Frames at 30 fps (1171 frames, 39.03 s); words from the approved narration (`production/NARRATION_APPROVED.txt`).
Shot code: `source/src/shots.tsx` (`SHOTS`). Climb event frames: `source/src/world/climb.ts` (`C`); bench event
frames: `source/src/world/bench.ts` (`BK`); both are exported with the shot boundaries to `audio/cues.json`.

Screen direction is fixed for the whole film: the glass is on the **left**, the climber on the right facing left,
"up" is up. The micro section keeps the same orientation (glass left, tile backing right, wedges leaning down toward
the glass), and so does the bench (specimen left, he works it from the right).
