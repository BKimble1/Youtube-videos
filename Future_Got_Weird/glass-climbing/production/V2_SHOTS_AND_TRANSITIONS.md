# Glass Climbing V2: shots, transitions and what moves on every arrival

Frames at 30 fps (1162 frames, 38.73 s). Narration: V1's approved take, word for word and sample for sample; only the
silent tail after "contact." is shorter (0.42 s, a sentence pause, so the line runs on into "If you pressed..." at the
loop). Shot code: `source/src/v2/shots2.tsx` (`SHOTS2`); the climb: `source/src/v2/climb2.ts` (`C`); the set:
`source/src/v2/set.ts`, `Set2.tsx`.

The set (new in V2): a glass curtain wall (mullions every 2.26 m, transoms every 1.6 m) standing on a brick knee wall,
drawn in the bay's oblique projection so the wall reads as a wall, with a step stool in front of the bricks. Screen
direction is fixed throughout: the wall on the left, the climber facing it from the right; the micro views keep it
(glass left, the pad's tiles right).
