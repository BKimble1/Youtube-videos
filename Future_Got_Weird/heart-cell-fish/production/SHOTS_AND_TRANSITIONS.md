## Transition list

Format: outgoing → incoming: new information. Decision.

1. **1 → 2.** Close on the muscle → the orbit (continuous, same fish, same swim track). New: the body is thin and empty. **Keep.**
2. **2 → 3.** Glass fish, side-B profile → the paper fish in tweezers: same screen place, size and direction (a
   match between two states of the object). New: it was built by hand, at bench scale. **Keep.**
3. **3 (out and back in).** A pull-back to the scientist, then a rush back to the hinge. New: the scale (14 mm), who
   made it, and where the cells grow. **Keep.** The return completes a step: it goes deeper than it started, into the tissue.
4. **3 → 4.** The hinge → the cells (a continuous zoom, the cells rising through the gelatin). New: what the muscle is. **Keep.**
5. **4 → 5.** Cells → fish side (a continuous zoom out; the cells shrink into the sheet). New: the cells are the sheet on the fish. **Keep.**
6. **5 → 6.** Side view → overhead (one rise). New: both sheets, the layers edge-on. **Keep.**
7. **6 → 7.** Overhead → in close on side B (a push). New: the stretch, the trigger. **Keep.**
8. **7 → 8 → 9.** Overhead → behind and below → low follow (one swing). New: the beat becomes swimming. **Keep.**
9. **9 → 10.** Low follow → side and glass (continuous). New: no motor on board. **Keep.**
10. **10 → 11.** A cut to the time-lapse: a new time. **Keep.**
11. **11 → 12.** Through the glass → out to the lab (one continuous pull-back; the tank's interior is drawn in 3D from
    the lab camera, so the view through the glass is the same picture). New: scale and the person. **Keep.**
12. **12 → 13.** Pan to the model → push into it. New: the goal. **Keep.**
13. **13 (model → living heart).** A cut in place, on "how": a match between a model and the organ, not a morph. **Keep.**
14. **13 → 14.** Heart wall → fish muscle (a match cut: same colour, same fibre direction, the beat continuing). New: the
    tissue is the same kind of muscle. **Keep.**
15. **14 → 1 (loop).** The pull-back lands on frame 0's camera, fish pose and swim phase (shot 14 replays the opening
    track at t − 30.03 s). **Keep.**

Nothing was removed: no shot returns to a previous subject without completing a step.
