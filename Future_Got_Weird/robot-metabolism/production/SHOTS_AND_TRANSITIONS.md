# V1 shots, setups and transitions

Times are measured from the final narration (30 fps, 771 frames, 25.70 s). Every action is placed with `T('phrase')`
from `source/src/lib/time.ts`, so it lands on its words. Four cuts; everything else is continuous camera movement.
