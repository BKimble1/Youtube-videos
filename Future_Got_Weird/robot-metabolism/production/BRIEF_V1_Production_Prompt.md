6. The final line is conditional. A nonfunctional assembly can contain usable compatible modules. Do not show a completely broken motor magically becoming operational.
7. No literal eating, mouths, teeth, robot combat, forced dismemberment, blood, organic tissue or humanoid anatomy.
8. The project’s contracted module is about 28 cm long. Keep the lab scale plausible; do not depict microscopic bots.
9. The film may combine separate demonstrated capabilities in a coherent explanatory sequence. Do not imply it recreates one uninterrupted laboratory trial.
