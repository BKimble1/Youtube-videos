This robot uses other robots as spare body parts.

It’s built from motorized rods that stretch and shrink, with magnets at their ends.

A person controls the movements, guiding another rod into place.

Once it connects, that extra piece becomes a walking stick, helping the robot move faster downhill.

And if a part stops working, it can drop it and attach a replacement.

So that broken robot beside it…
could become its next body part.
