What the scene does: it turns the film from the problem to the remedies. Inside the S3 machine room it shows three partial helps. (1) Retrieval brings the real record (Kalai's actual thesis title block) into the EVIDENCE CHECK booth, which was closed in S3. (2) Reasoning helps "sometimes". (3) Turning down the randomness dial makes answers identical, but they are still WRONG. It ends on NO GUARANTEE, which sets up S9's "check." The story beats are right; the execution is the problem.

Biggest execution problems:
(1) Still for 53% of the runtime (7.6 of 14.2 s, seven still runs). Each help is one event followed by a freeze. The longest freeze is 1.63 s (f6322–6370), and it falls exactly on "when they bring the real record into the room."
(2) The retrieval beat is broken. The RETRIEVAL cart is drawn behind the ASSEMBLY machine and only shows for about 0.37 s (f6304–6315). The real record hangs below the cart deck across the track (world y 705–870) instead of riding on it. The cart then vanishes in mid-air and the record teleports into the booth. All of this is finished before "bring the real record" is spoken.
(3) The camera points at the wrong thing. During "Reasoning, sometimes." the camera stays in the BOOTH framing, so the thinking machine is cut by the left edge and its label reads "omness". The DIAL framing crops the booth header to "EVIDENCE CHE" for 4.7 s. The final pull-back to WIDE happens at the same moment the NO GUARANTEE stamp lands, and it shrinks the WRONG stamps.
(4) The consistency payoff can't be seen. The three "identical" slips fade in where they stand, stacked in mid-air between the two machines and hanging below the track. Two of the three are about 80% covered, nothing marks them as the same, and the WRONG stamps are 30 px and overlap each other.
(5) Phone readability. Almost every supporting text falls to 5–10 px at 640x360: the record title, both chips, the bubble chip, the dial labels, the slip text and the WRONG stamps. NO GUARANTEE, the scene's conclusion, is only 44 px and sits on the floor strip under another chip.
