# V1 shot review — S8 — What helps (s28). V1 frames 6232–6659 (3:27.73–3:41.97, 14.2 s; mounted f6226–6665 for the wipes). Source /home/user/Youtube-videos/Future_Got_Weird/Video_01_Pass_2/source/src/scenes/S8_Helps.tsx

What the scene does: it turns the film from the problem to the remedies. In the blue machine room from S3 (the wall, pipe and track) it shows three partial helps. (1) Retrieval brings the real record, Kalai's actual thesis title block, into the EVIDENCE CHECK booth. S2 showed that booth shut ("a different job · not on this route"); S3 never had it. (2) Reasoning helps "sometimes". (3) Turning the randomness dial down makes the machine give the same answer every time, and that answer is still WRONG. The scene ends on NO GUARANTEE, which sets up S9's "check." The story beats are right; the execution fails.

Biggest execution problems:
(1) Still for 53% of the runtime (7.6 of 14.2 s, across seven runs of ≥ 0.8 s). The longest is 1.63 s (f6322–6370), on "when they bring the real record into the room." Some stretches move only by an opacity fade. Counting those, the booth shot is effectively frozen for f6323–6398 (2.5 s) and the dial/slip shot for f6485–6570 (2.8 s).
(2) Retrieval is broken, and it finishes too early. The cart runs behind ASSEMBLY at up to ≈139 px/frame. The whole cart is on screen for only 4 frames (f6309–6312). Its record dangles below the deck, across the track and the floor line, and from f6301 it slides in under ASSEMBLY before any carrier is visible. The wheels float ≈14 px above the track and never visibly turn. The cart vanishes in mid-move over the booth at f6313, and by then the record is already inside the booth. All of this is over before "bring the real record" is spoken.
(3) The evidence reads as crossed out. The shutter slat lines stay drawn on the open window to the end of the scene, and one of them runs exactly through the title line "Probabilistic and On-line Methods". With the sound off, the real record looks struck through. The title is also only ≈13 px tall at 1080p.
(4) The camera points at the wrong thing and moves badly. The BOOTH framing crops ASSEMBLY to "omness" during the reasoning line. The DIAL framing crops the booth to "EVIDENCE CHE" for 4.7 s. The BOOTH→DIAL move whips at 135 px/frame on "Turning", with a zoom hiccup. Screen-space text (the sign and the chip) slides against the world during camera moves, and the final pull-back fights the verdict.
(5) The consistency payoff and the conclusions don't read. The slips fade in as a diagonal pile between the machines: two of the three are ≈80% hidden, and the front one is jammed against the real record. The WRONG stamps are 30 px and step into one another like a staircase. Both conclusion texts are crammed onto the floor strip, and NO GUARANTEE is 44 px with its bottom edge 52 px from the frame edge.
