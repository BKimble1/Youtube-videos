# V1 shot review — S7 — Benchmarks (real Table 2, tally 9/10, leaderboard), narration s26–s27, V1 frames 5698–6232 (mounted 5692–6238 with wipe overlap), 3:09.9–3:27.7, 17.8 s

What the scene does: it is the hinge from the illustrative game show (S6) to real evidence. The real Table 2 from Kalai, Nachum, Vempala & Zhang (2025) shows that 9 of the 10 benchmarks they sampled (mid-2025) grade strictly right or wrong and give no credit for "I don't know". The scene then draws the consequence (train and rank on tests like that, and guessing pays) with an illustrative leaderboard and the S6 trophy, and closes on the honesty caveat ("one explanation, not the whole story… but a simple one").

Biggest execution problems in V1:
1) Dead holds. 10.7 of 17.8 s (60%) is completely still, and the longest still run is 3.13 s. Each cue fires one outline box or one pop, finishes in about 0.5 s and freezes. The frame is fully frozen through "ten widely used benchmarks in mid-2025" (5751–5840, 3.0 s), through "no credit at all for 'I don't know.' Train" (5924–5986, 2.1 s), through "models on tests like that, and guessing pays" (6024–6071, 1.6 s) and through the whole caveat (6112–6205, 3.1 s).
2) The evidence is shown as one flat page. Table 2 sits at one fixed size (row glyphs about 20 px, about 7 px on a phone) with no zoom into the title, the date or the entries. "mid-2025" exists only in a 22 px grey citation chip. On "Train" the table even shrinks by 14%, so the text gets smaller just when the argument needs it. Large parts of the frame stay empty: the right 27% until 5842, the lower-right quadrant until 5996, and the band at y≈800–960 for the whole scene.
3) The argument is not staged, so it is unreadable muted. The counter counts 1→9 after the narrator has already said "Nine". The WildBench exception is a 4-frame ink row box plus a 22 px grey footnote, while both column highlights still enclose WildBench's "No"/"Partial", so the boxes visually say 10/10. Nothing links the coral IDK column to the leaderboard. The ranking is never performed. The trophy lands nowhere.
4) Layout breakage at the payoff. The "guessing pays · illustration" chip covers "· CC BY 4.0" in the citation tag for the last 5.4 s, which hides a factual guard rail. The trophy is cropped by the right frame edge and floats in mid-air. The leaderboard carries no "illustration" label for its first 2.5 s.
5) The transitions are weak. The wipe in reveals an empty set, and the table pops in after the wipe instead of travelling from the S6 scoreboard. The exit stacks an S7 opacity fade under S8's hard wipe, which leaves a half-faded ghost table at 6232.
