# V1 shot review — S4 — Library (s13–s16), V1 frames 2557–3618 (1:25.2–2:00.6), 35.0 s, 64% still

What S4 does for the story: it is the "why" of the episode and links S3's token machine to S5's actual record. The model learned from shelves full of text shaped like dissertation titles ("Methods." "Algorithms." "Machine Learning."). One specific researcher's title shows up rarely or never, and like a birthday there is no pattern to work it out from. So the model fills the gap with a title-shaped answer, and the confident wording ("is entitled", no "I think", no "maybe") comes from the same pattern. The props are right: shelves, a card catalogue, a card with a blank "title:" line, the empty slot with the birthday cake, the ChatGPT slip, the IS ENTITLED seal and the struck-out hedge words. The execution is not.

Biggest problems:
(1) Pop, then freeze. 22.4 of 35.0 s is still (64%), across 13 still runs. The longest are 2.7 s on "likely answer be wrong? Think about what the model learned from" (f2611–2691), 2.57 s on "because that's part of the pattern too" (f3409–3485), 2.2 s on "specific researcher's title? That might show up rarely" (f3020–3085) and 2.17 s on "rarely, or not at all. And like a birthday" (f3093–3157). There is no background life at all, so every hold is 100% frozen.
(2) The library mechanics the brief calls out are broken. The drawer front slides 150 px sideways over its neighbour and leaves a dark rectangle that reads as an empty hole (f3022–3235). The KALAI card fades in floating above the cabinet top, touching nothing. The clerk's hand stops at the hole, not on a handle. There is no searching, and no card coming out of the drawer.
(3) Staging and camera.
   - In the shelf push-ins the clerk is cut by the right edge (f2743–2815) and then at chest height by the bottom edge (f2815–2904).
   - From f3337 to the end (about 9.4 s) the 470 px slip covers his head and torso. The "confident clerk" is faceless exactly during the confidence beat.
   - The gap (far left), the card (top right) and the clerk (centre) are spread across the frame.
   - The camera goes back and forth: WIDE → SHELF_A → SHELF_B → WIDE → CAB → SLIPCAM.
(4) Continuity breaks at "So the model…". Every s15 element vanishes within about 12 frames (f3231–3247): the drawer, card, gap outline, cake, chips and footer. Then 1.93 s of empty medium shot. So "It fills the gap" fills nothing the viewer has seen: five chips fly from fixed points (one starts off-screen) to the clerk's chest, vanish on arrival, and a ready-made slip fades in over them with visible ghosting (f3342).
(5) Phone readability.
   - The catalogue card, which is the beat's focal point ("KALAI, A. · 2001", "title:"), is about 8–9 px on a 640x360 phone.
   - The model-label line "GPT-4o · published excerpt" is about 7 px.
   - Both honesty footers are about 9 px, and the birthday footer is a single 1180 px line.
