The beats and the joke are right; the execution is not.

The five biggest problems:

(3) The comparison is unreadable on a phone and with the sound muted:
- The page shrinks to 700 px, so its evidence lines are about 14 px tall (about 5 px on a phone).
- No slip fragment is ever placed next to its matching page line, so '2002 vs 2001' is never shown side by side.
