# V1 shot review — S3 — The token machine (conveyor, NEXT-CHUNK SCORER, roll-out, likely vs true, add-on modules). Narration s08–s12. V1 global frames 1331–2563 (0:44.4–1:25.4) plus a 6-frame overlap into S4's wipe (to 2569).

What S3 does for the story: it opens the explanation. It takes ChatGPT's wrong answer from the cold open and shows the mechanism behind it. Text is built from tokens (real GPT-4o o200k_base tiles, with "Kalai" split into "Kal"+"ai"). A scorer ranks candidate next chunks and picks one, the answer rolls out piece by piece, and the scores measure likelihood, not truth. Add-ons (instruction training, reasoning, sometimes web search) don't change the piece-by-piece process. This is the setup for "likely ≠ true", which S4 to S10 build on.

Biggest execution problems:
(1) It is static 70% of the time: 28.8 of 41.1 s have nothing changing. The longest hold is 3.8 s (f2054–2167, "Here, it's choosing the last digit of the year. Those scores say which chunk is likely."). Almost all real motion comes from four camera moves, not from the machine.
(2) The conveyor never conveys and the cycle is not shown. The picked tile spins 360° inside the panel, misses the chute, vanishes about 350 world px left of the row end and teleports into the sentence. During the roll-out the scorer sits dead with a stale list. Tokens pop in at the right end while the whole row jumps 70 px per token with no easing.
(3) Cropping and the camera moving away from the information. From the split at 0:49 to the end of the scene the right end of the sentence is off-screen ("complet", "disser", "entitle" cut; ":" off-frame). Exactly when "Token after token, an answer rolls out" starts, the camera pulls back to WIDE, and the new tokens land past the right frame edge.
(4) The story logic of s11 is broken. At "Here, it's choosing the last digit of the year" the sentence already reads "…200 2 at CM U ) is entitled :". The scorer hard-swaps back to the digit list. Nothing links the scorer's "2" to the "2" tile.
(5) Readability, layout and sound. The guard-rail chip "illustrative numbers" is about 7 px on a phone. The 5th candidate row overflows the scorer panel. The s11 cards hug the left frame edge at 35–40% opacity for 4–6 s. The s12 modules float in the sky instead of bolting onto the machine. Three "piece by piece" tile clicks (f2526–2536) play over a picture where nothing happens, because `pieceT` is computed but never used.
