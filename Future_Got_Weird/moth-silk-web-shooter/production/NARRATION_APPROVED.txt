Imagine firing liquid at a criminal—and watching it turn into a web that catches them.

Scientists have taken the first step toward that.

They start with silk from moth cocoons and dissolve it into a liquid.

As it shoots through a special nozzle, a surrounding layer of acetone triggers the silk to harden.

A sticky fiber forms in midair.

It hits the metal and attaches.

Then you pull—and the blade lifts out of the sand.

In lab tests, these fibers lifted over eighty times their own weight.

The beginnings of a real web shooter… made from moth silk.
