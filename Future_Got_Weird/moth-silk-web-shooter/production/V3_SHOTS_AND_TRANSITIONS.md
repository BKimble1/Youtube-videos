## One-second audit (what develops)

0-1 aim completes, trigger, release, flight · 1-2 contact, web spreads, strand tight, jolt · 2-3 cut to the device,
fingers relax · 3-4 push begins, window grows · 4-5 the solution and its strands fill the frame · 5-6 into the syringe,
pull-back starts · 6-7 pump, beaker, hand, face · 7-8 hand to the cocoon dish · 8-9 pinch, lift, fibres draw out ·
9-10 carried, set on the liquid, released · 10-11 rod gripped, stirring, cocoon dissolving · 11-12 button pressed,
solution drawn up · 12-13 flow along the tube, cutaway opens, silk enters the core · 13-14 acetone enters the outer
channel · 14-15 both channels flowing · 15-16 the core hardens at the outlet, vapour · 16-17 the fibre leaves ·
17-18 the blade established · 18-19 closing on the blade end · 19-20 contact, slack draws out · 20-21 regrip, pull,
taut · 21-22 the end rises · 22-23 sand falls, blade free · 23-24 the junction close · 24-25 up the fibre · 25-26 the
