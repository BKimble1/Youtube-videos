## Known and not fixed in D2 (kit-side or by design)

* **Wreck poses (MECH):** `mech/unit.tsx` `restAngle` (R4 WIP) now stops the topple at the first probe contact: measured rotation 12.8 deg
  for B (kneeling; R3: 86 deg, "down on its face"), 47.2 deg for D (R3: 83.5), A 82.8, C 86. B and D therefore rest propped:
  pelvis 0.68 / 0.82 m and head 1.28 / 1.55 m above the ground at 116.92 (probed). Ask MECH: a knee on the ground must not be
  a limiting probe, or give `fall` an opt-out. I did not edit mech/*.
* **Cable through C (108.43-109.17):** C stands on A's drag line, so the cable from A's clamp to post 3 passes through C's
  chest (3 cm from its centre). Hidden behind both machines in every camera; fx/tether has no wrap-around.
* **A and C do not touch the girder:** at the crash the closest approach of any mesh vertex to any truss member is 0.83 m (C,
  right pauldron); A 1.6 m. No limb passes through a member (probed, 108.0-110.2, every frame, member boxes vs sampled mesh
  edges). The FX are placed at the chord and the camera sees the robots overlap it in projection, so it reads as a hit.
* Small remaining person/robot overlaps (<= 5 cm): runner's left shin vs D's wreck foot 115.33-116.9.
* Foot checks: a probe flags planted-and-moving feet and one-frame jumps for all seven crew roles and all machines at 30 fps over 94.30-116.92. Only the runner step above slid. Feet "jumps" of 45-60 cm per frame are the swing legs of runs/walks, not teleports.
