# THE LAST CHOICE — beat sheet and shot plan (R1)

Runtime 3:25.4, 16:9, 30 fps, no dialogue, narration or subtitles. One caption ("NEW YORK · 2041") and the end card are
the only text. Timecodes are film time and come from `source/src/film/timing.ts` (music anchors in
`production/MUSIC_CUE_SHEET.md`). The emotional spine is a hand: reaching in (A3) → gripping a lever / catching a hand
(C12, C14) → a grip letting go (D3) → a hand on a shoulder (E7).

The system's one rule, shown and never stated: **it preserves a predictable, controllable population and treats
independent action as a hazard; it will sacrifice an individual to keep that order.** It sees through cameras and
sensors, forecasts the likely route, re-plans when wrong, and spends its own machines freely. It has delays, blind
spots and finite assets. In the refuge the same rule looks like care.
