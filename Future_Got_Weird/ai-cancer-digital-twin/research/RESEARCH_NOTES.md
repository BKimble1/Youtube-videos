Details drawn from general knowledge rather than the brief (not re-checked here): the DNA switch displays a
hexagonal arrangement of six death-receptor-binding peptides; its mouse comparison was against an inactive
version of the structure. The brief states the comparison ("reduced tumor growth compared with an inactive
device") but not the hexagon. The device's shape on screen is a stylised clamshell of helix bundles, informed by
the published schematic as described, not a structural model.
