# Flying Skateboard V3: shots and transitions

The narration, word timings, captions and length are the same as V1 and V2: 30 fps, 1077 frames, 35.900 s.
- Shot table: `SHOTS3` in `source/src/v3/shots3.tsx`.
- Flight models: `source/src/v3/flight3.ts`.
- Composition: `FlyingSkateboardV3`.

Every change of place is either a continuous camera move, a motivated match (one moving thing becomes the next), or a cut to a different view of the same action. There are no wipes, no whip pans and no camera shake.

## Hook breakdown (shot 1)

| Time (s) | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.00–0.45 | Frame 0 is already mid-crossing. The camera is slightly below and ahead of the board; behind him are the river, the near-bank beach and the cliffs. The ten fans spin and the deck has a small roll; his knees and shoulders are mid-correction. |
| 0.45–1.15 | The camera moves alongside him. The bank and the downstream bridge slide behind him, and the water streams below. |
| 1.15–1.90 | The camera moves down and slightly behind the board: the ten-fan underside, the stator arms and the battery, with the rotors turning and the air leaving swept back by the forward speed. |
| 1.90–2.65 | The camera rises to a high three-quarter view. His shadow lies on the water far below, so the height reads; he leans and the board corrects. He stays large in frame. |
| 2.65–3.41 | A lateral follow with the far bank, town and cliffs ahead, then the camera closes in under the board. |
| 3.41–6.42 | The ten duct rims tick as they are counted. He comes down to about 4 m, so the downwash visibly roughens the water. On "electric propellers" the rotors spin up and the air jets lengthen. On "blast air downward" the camera pushes into one fan until the spinning disc fills the frame. |

## Continuity locks

- **One board** (`BD` in `art/Board.tsx`): ten ducts in two rows of five, the same geometry in every shot. V3 adds the stator arms seen from below and the artificial horizon on the deck phone.
- **One pilot** (`art/rig.ts`, `art/Pilot.tsx`):
  - The same helmet, visor, jacket, gloves, shoes and proportions in every shot.
  - Both ankles are planted on the deck in every shot, with two-bone IK legs and arms.
  - The rider's-eye view now draws his full legs, so they don't pop in when the camera leaves the helmet.
- **One river set** for the hook, the riverside flat, the crossing, the canyon and the ending:
  - V3 adds the riverside flat between the water and the near-bank hills (`env/Shore.tsx`) and limestone cliff bands on the ridges.
  - The gorge is built into the set (`env/Gorge.tsx`).
