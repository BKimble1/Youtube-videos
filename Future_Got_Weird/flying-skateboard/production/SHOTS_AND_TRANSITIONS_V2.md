# Flying Skateboard V2: shots and transitions

Same narration, word timings and length as V1: 30 fps, 1077 frames, 35.900 s. The shot table is `SHOTS2` in
`source/src/v2/shots2.tsx`, the flight models are in `source/src/v2/flight2.ts`, and every boundary sits on a measured word (`T('phrase')`).
Composition: `FlyingSkateboardV2`.

V2 has no wipes; the V1 rock-spur and mist wipes are gone. Every change of place is either a continuous camera move, a
motivated match (one moving thing becomes the next), or a cut to a different view of the same action.

## Continuity locks

- **One board** (`BD` in `art/Board.tsx`): ten ducts in two rows of five, at x = ±0.285 m and z = −0.52 … 0.52 m. It is used in every shot, the cutaway and the bench build included.
- **One pilot** (`art/rig.ts`, `art/Pilot.tsx`): the same helmet, visor, orange jacket, gloves, shoes and proportions. The feet are planted at the same deck positions in every shot, with two-bone IK legs and arms. The V2 rig adds hip lead, head levelling, balance arms, listening and controller use.
- **One river set** for the hook, the glide, the payoff and the ending. The gorge is built into it (`env/Gorge.tsx`), so the canyon flight is one connected path rather than a separate set.
