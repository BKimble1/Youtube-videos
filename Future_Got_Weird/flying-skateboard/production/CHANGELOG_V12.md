## Opening, 0–3 s
- **Frame 0:** he is already over the gravel bar's upstream edge, so the stones and the bar's edge stream past from the first frame. He and the ten-rotor board are large, with the river below.
- **0.45–1.4 s:** tracking alongside down to deck level: the water beneath him and the far bank ahead.
- **1.4–2.6 s:** a purposeful widening to the crossing and the far bank (bridge, town) as he advances. He stays mid-size.
- **Then** down under the board into the mechanism.

## Failure, 10.6–12.5 s
- **A local fire:** it starts at the centre engine and reaches only its nearest neighbour (V11: all five).
- **The equipment reacts:** the stand jolts as the fire catches, and the engines spool down. Smoke builds.
- **The match cut** to the electric fan is unchanged.

## The problem, 14.7–16.4 s
- **The sequence:**
  1. The board tips.
  2. He answers too hard: his weight goes too far, and the board swings past level the other way.
  3. It sinks toward the ground (about 0.45 m) while off axis.
  4. He recovers.
- **The view:** angled more toward the nose, so the deck's tilt reads across the frame while his body shift shows. The crane up into the top-down view is kept.
- **Checked:** feet planted on every frame (0.00 mm), knees never bent backward.

## The coach
- **A larger error:** the steady error the deck phone reads is now about 7° (V11: 4.8°), so his coached correction visibly levels the board.
- **The deck phone:** its attitude readout is shown in the legs close-up ("He has to control it") and in the correction view.
- **Tried and reverted:** opening the coach view on the deck phone. Seen from his shoulder it put his legs across the phone, the crowding the brief rules out.

## Achievement, 25.4–28.6 s
- **The crossing:** the camera starts alongside him as the shore falls behind, then swings round behind him: open water beneath, the far bank approaching.
- **The canyon:** unchanged from V11, opening close and swinging out to reveal the walls.

## Question and loop
- **Under the question:** he flies with the far bank in view (V11: empty water).
- **The loop:** the camera then rises onto frame 0's view. The gravel bar's edge enters as the loop closes, so the seam is continuous.
