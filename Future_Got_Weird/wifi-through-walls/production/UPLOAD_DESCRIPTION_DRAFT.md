**Title idea:** Wi-Fi Can See Through Walls 😳 (alternatives: "This Machine Can Watch You Through Walls", "Your Wi-Fi Knows Who You Are")

**Description:**

Scientists can track your body through a wall with radio waves, and Wi-Fi can recognize people walking past, even
without their phones. Would you rather have a camera watching you, or Wi-Fi that knows who you are? 👇

Sources:
• MIT CSAIL, RF-Pose: Zhao et al., "Through-Wall Human Pose Estimation Using Radio Signals," CVPR 2018 — https://rfpose.csail.mit.edu/
• KIT, Todt, Morsbach & Strufe, "BFId: Identity Inference Attacks Utilizing Beamforming Feedback Information," ACM CCS 2025 — https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000185756
• KIT press release 069/2025 — https://www.kit.edu/kit/english/pi_2025_069_the-spy-who-came-in-from-the-wifi-beware-of-radio-network-surveillance.php

Two separate studies. RF-Pose estimates a stick-figure skeleton with specialized research radio hardware, not a camera
image. BFId re-identified previously recorded volunteers in a controlled lab study (197 people recorded); it does not
learn names. The café scenes are a hypothetical illustration.
