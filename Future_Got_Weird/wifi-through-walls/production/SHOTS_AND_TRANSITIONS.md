# Wi-Fi Through Walls V1: shots and transitions

Times are from the measured narration (`audio/narration/words_final.json`). Film: 1094 frames, 36.47 s, 30 fps.
Every camera is a pinhole camera in one set's metre space (`source/src/world/cam.ts`); sets are flat vector planes;
characters are the Weird Crew guesser's rig (`source/src/dc/`) placed as cards at their floor point.
