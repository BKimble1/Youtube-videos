# JIZAI ARMS V1: shots and transitions

The film runs 31.0 s (930 frames at 30 fps). Times are film seconds, aligned to the narration master's word times
(`audio/narration/FGW_JIZAI_words_V1.json`). The whole film is one 3D world: the camera is a real orbit or dolly
camera, and every person, arm and prop is fixed in that world.

**Cuts.** There are three:

- **18.5 s:** a geometric match cut.
- **21.6 s:** a deliberate cut to a separate demonstration.
- **31.0 → 0:** the loop.

Everything else is continuous camera motion.
