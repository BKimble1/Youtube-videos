6. LIQUID-METAL ANIMATION ENGINE

This is the single most important technical improvement after the hook.

V1’s metallic character needs substantially better deformation.

Implement the strongest feasible approach within the existing Remotion pipeline.

Consider:

* SVG path interpolation
* Procedural Bézier geometry
* Layered liquid silhouettes
* Mesh deformation where supported
* Particle-based droplets
* Shape-preserving interpolation
* Animated reflective highlight masks
* Controlled motion trails
* Custom transformation keyframes

Do not introduce heavy simulation dependencies unless they materially improve the final rendered result.

Physical animation requirements

Liquid material must appear to conserve volume.

The character must visibly compress through openings instead of shrinking arbitrarily.

Metal should stretch into continuous streams, not disconnect into unrelated floating pieces.

Droplets must merge back into the primary mass.

Reflections must move consistently with the changing surface.

When the robot reforms, the transformation must be continuous.

Performance requirements

Avoid expensive effects that cause rendering failures.

Use deterministic animation.

Keep motion smooth at the project’s established frame rate.

Ensure every rendered frame has valid geometry.

No flashing polygons, disconnected limbs, or broken masks.
