# Invisible Touch V4: V3 audit and the five most visible fixes

This audit was done before editing. It worked from the V3 render
(`exports/FGW_Invisible_Touch_V3_REVIEW_1080x1920.mp4`), stepped at the cited windows and at both sides of every cut,
against the measured word onsets in `source/src/data/timeline.json`. The narration and voice are unchanged.

## The five most visible fixes (in priority order)

1. **Hook (0–1.9 s).**
   - The finger *reaches in*: frame 0 is mid-motion.
   - At contact, a **skin-localised tactile pulse**: a bright point on the pad and ripples clipped to the finger's own
     surface, with two faint crests arriving from below.
   - An involuntary flinch and a subtle click at the moment of contact.
   - The parallax reveal of the empty gap by 1.0–1.8 s.
2. **3.2–7.2 s: three distinct, purposeful views** in place of four near-repeats.
   - Hand and device in one high three-quarter frame showing the gap.
   - A low path beneath the finger.
   - A clean dive down into the array.
3. **The array reads as science, not a keyboard (7–11 s).**
   - Round transducer cans with curved silhouettes, recessed seats, consistent spacing and subtle depth, so no square
     keycaps.
   - Specific emitters fire in a coordinated pattern.
   - **No laser rays**: only converging wavefronts to one spot at the skin.
4. **Skin beat (11.2–14.3 s): dive *into the same fingertip*.**
   - The camera pushes into the pad at the focal spot.
   - The finger's own silhouette is opened locally: skin layers follow the finger's contour, with receptors at the
     focus and a nerve fibre along the finger.
   - The camera pulls back out on the same axis. No full-screen panel.
5. **Car micro-story and the loop.**
   - **Car:** one unmistakable temperature interaction in a single shot (reach, pulse, the dial and the cabin light
     respond), fewer icons, and every floating control directly above the console array (a clear path).
   - **Ending:** a pinch on the imaginary button that closes through empty air, then a final pulse on the skin.
   - **Loop:** the hand retreats and reaches in again at the opening's exact motion, and the first caption is
     pre-built over the last frames, so the seam is continuous.

Also in scope, as the brief asks:

- **14.3–19.2 s:** the moving sensation becomes an A → B → C mini-demonstration, in one overhead view and one oblique
  tracking view, each with a local response at every stop.
- **Captions:** checked at every transition and at the seam.
