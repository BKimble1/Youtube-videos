If you stuck a needle into a DEAD spider… you could make its legs MOVE again.
Because when a spider dies, all its legs curl inward.
You see, spiders use fluid pressure to straighten their legs.
And when they die, that pressure disappears.
So scientists attached a syringe to a dead spider… and pumped air inside.
Its legs suddenly spread OPEN.
But when they released the pressure… the legs CLAMPED SHUT.
They'd turned a dead spider into a working claw.
And they actually used it to pick up… ANOTHER dead spider.
