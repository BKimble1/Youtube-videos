## Required footage plan

1. **0:00–0:15:** Real 2026 JR East operation, ideally an authorized excerpt showing branch gripped by claw while chainsaw cuts. Real cutting sequence should be continuous and credible. News lower thirds and watermarks must not be deceptively obscured; attribution and extent of use depend on rights review.
2. **0:15–0:30:** Same machine mounted on rail-capable truck and boom, operator / cab. Do not cut to the 2024 JR West machine as if identical.
3. **0:30–1:15:** Authorized official JR West or JR East operation footage when available; supplement with **original FGW** clean 2D/2.5D mechanical reconstructions and clear 'illustration' labels for any reconstructed sequence.
4. **1:15–2:00:** Original mechanical animations of bilateral control are preferred. Important: this capability is documented for JR West's system; do not imply identical force-feedback specifications for JR East's exact October 2026 machine without verification.
5. **2:00–2:30:** Original animated illustration of railway work at height; do not invent a news accident clip.
6. **2:30–3:00:** JR West October 2026 machine visuals and rollout, distinguish the new **October single-arm** vs planned **November humanoid** model; ideally licensed stills or footage.
7. **3:00–end:** Return to the permitted real operation shot; visually connect the human controls to the two robotic arms.

## Rights workflow

- Before ingest, record: source owner; media URL; title; original publication date; license/permission or specific editorial-use assessment; permitted channels/territories/duration; required credits; expiration; downloaded filename; SHA-256 and local path; intended timecodes.
- **Never treat availability on YouTube or a corporate website as a license.** Attribution is not a substitute for permission. Fair-use assessment (if relevant) is individualized; no automatic safe number of seconds.
- Do not hotlink undocumented video streams or strip platform watermarks.
- Use original FGW vector reconstructions for shots that cannot be cleared. Do not call reconstruction 'real footage'.
- If usable real footage cannot be cleared, flag it explicitly and generate a *previsualization only*, with conspicuous source-footage placeholders, rather than mislabeling a final as publication-ready.

## Verified factual anchors

- JR East October 2026 media shows the machine operated near Nobeyama on the Koumi Line; March 2026 introduction. Source: KAB/ANN; FNN.
- JR West deployed related humanoid heavy equipment from July 2024. Source: JR West, October 2026 press release.
- JR West October 16, 2026 release: new single-arm lifting robot up to **120 kg**, entering operation in **October**; updated humanoid model intended for tree clearing, entering operation in **November 2026**; target workforce reduction of approximately **30%** for relevant tasks, not established measured outcome.
