## Transition list (outgoing subject → incoming subject → new information → decision)

1. **S1 → S2, 7.00 s, cut.** The treated patch → the same patch at the same framing, earlier, still opaque, being
   rubbed with the solution → *a genuinely new time*: what was done, and that it was not instant. **Keep**
   as a matched cut. The EARLIER cue marks the time jump.
2. **S2 → S3, 9.83–10.63 s, call-out.** The patch → a schematic section of that patch → *reveals the mechanism*.
   **Keep.** The ring stays on the patch, and the leader lines tie the section to that spot. The patch stays visible below.
3. **S3 → S4, 19.95–20.45 s, call-out closes.** The section → the same patch, now clear → *completes the
   explanation and advances the action* (rinse). **Keep.** The explanation is complete before the return. It is
   not an excursion that returns without new information.
4. **Loop junction, 25.07 → 0.00 s.** Opaque skin at z 1.18 → clear skin at z 1.10 → a compositional restart, not
   a seamless loop. **Keep.** The narration does not loop grammatically.

There are no other cuts. There are no whips, spins, flashes, blur dissolves or crossfades used to hide a discontinuity.
