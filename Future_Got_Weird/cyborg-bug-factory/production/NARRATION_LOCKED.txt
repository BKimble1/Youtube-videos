Scientists built a factory that turns living cockroaches into robots. And it only takes 68 seconds. A robotic arm grabs a tiny computer and attaches it to the cockroach’s back. Then tiny electrodes deliver electrical pulses that can make the bug turn. So now, instead of building tiny robots from scratch… Scientists can use actual living insects. And here’s the crazy part. They’ve already sent cyborg cockroaches into a real earthquake disaster zone. Which means we’re not just building robots anymore. We’re turning bugs into them.
