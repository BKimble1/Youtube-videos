**Title:** These Contacts Let You See With Your Eyes Closed

**Description:**

Researchers built soft contact lenses filled with tiny particles that turn invisible near-infrared light into light
you can see. Because infrared passes through eyelids better than visible light, people wearing them could spot
infrared flashes even with their eyes shut.

The train scene is an illustration of a possible use, not part of the study.

Research: Ma, Xue et al., "Near-infrared spatiotemporal color vision in humans enabled by upconversion contact lenses,"
Cell (published 22 May 2025). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.04.019
University of Science and Technology of China: https://en.ustc.edu.cn/info/1007/5053.htm
