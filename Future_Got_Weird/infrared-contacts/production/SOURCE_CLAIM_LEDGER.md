## Boundaries kept in the artwork

- Closed-eye perception is shown only as diffuse flashes against darkness: no faces, text or room.
- Near-infrared perception only: no thermal or X-ray imagery. The transmitter is a deliberate, sufficiently strong NIR
  source (the lenses are not reported to pick up weak ambient infrared).
- The lens is a passive transparent soft lens: no battery, display, wires or glow of its own.
- Nanoparticles are in the lens only (the earlier mouse work injected particles under the retina; that is a different
  intervention and is not shown).
- Conversion happens in the lens, never in the eyelid.
- Opening the eyes still gives normal vision (the B diagram shows ordinary visible light reaching the retina).
- The supplementary glasses system the researchers built for finer images is outside this Short.
