**Transition types.**
- **direct cut**: the idea or the view changes.
- **cut on action**: the same action continues across the edit at the same phase (states are shared).
- **composition match**: the subject keeps its screen position and scale while the identity or place changes.

No dissolves, no wipes, and no swish moves between shots. Camera moves inside a shot are single, constant-speed
pushes or one eased lateral reveal (the mirror). The two-shot opening and callback use the same camera.
