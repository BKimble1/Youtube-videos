Each line is: outgoing subject → incoming subject → new information → keep, merge or remove. Times come from
`source/src/story.ts` (`EV`), keyed to the narration's measured word times. This is the final cut (V3).

There are no exterior → interior → exterior excursions that return without new information. Beats 1–7 make one
explanation: inside (reveal), the method (X-ray), the start (rewind), the route (backing up), the exit (cutaway),
then the outside again, where it began.
