If this sea slug cuts off its own HEAD… that head can keep CRAWLING.

And its headless BODY? It keeps MOVING too.

You see, there’s a weak spot just behind its head. When it breaks apart, even its HEART stays behind.

Scientists think it may be escaping PARASITES in its old body.

But the head starts eating algae… and about a week later, a NEW HEART starts BEATING.

Then, over the next few weeks, an ENTIRE NEW BODY grows back.

And one of these sea slugs actually did it… TWICE.
