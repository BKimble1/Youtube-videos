**Title (working):** This Shield Can Make You Disappear.

**Description:**

> Raise a panel covered in tiny vertical lenses and, from the right spot in front of you, your body can seem to melt
> into the wall behind you. The lenses spread your light sideways and smear the background across where you're
> standing — so a hand reaching round the edge looks like it's floating in midair.
>
> It's a passive optical trick, not a cloak: it works best viewed head-on against plain, horizontally lined
> backgrounds, and the panel's edges and some blur stay visible.
>
> Inspired by the lens-array shields made by Invisibility Shield Co. (UK), as shown in their Kickstarter campaigns and
> press coverage. The animation is a simplified illustration.
>
