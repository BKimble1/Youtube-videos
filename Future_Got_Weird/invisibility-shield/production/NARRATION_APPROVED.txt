If you raised this shield, someone looking straight at you could watch your body disappear.

You see, its surface is covered in tiny lenses.

They spread the light coming from your body sideways, while smearing the background across the space where you’re standing.

From the right position, your outline disappears into a blurred patch of scenery.

So you could reach one hand around the edge…

and make it look like a hand is floating in midair.

Then lower the shield…

and reveal you were standing right in front of them the entire time.
