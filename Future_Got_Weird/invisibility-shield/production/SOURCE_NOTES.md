## Facts the depiction relies on (as reported)

- **Passive.** A precision lens array: polymer sheet with fine, elongate, convex lenses running vertically. There is
  no power, display or electronics.
- **How it works (maker's simplified description).** The vertical lenses spread light from the narrow subject behind
  the panel into a diffuse horizontal smear, away from the observer. Light from the broader background is refracted
  across the panel toward the observer, so the background appears where the person is.
- **2.0 Full Size panel.** About 99 × 69 cm, clear polycarbonate, with ergonomic handles and a supporting frame so it
  can stand freely. Larger and smaller sizes exist: Mini about 20 cm; Megashield about 183 cm, for several people.
- **Works best:**
  - against uniform backgrounds with horizontal structure (horizon, walls, rails, painted lines);
  - in even or overcast light;
  - for an observer roughly in front of the panel.
- **Vertical background features blur away**, because the lenses are vertical.
- **Limitations reported:**
  - a faint outline and blurring can remain;
  - movement shows through;
  - the effect depends on angle;
  - the user cannot see clearly through the panel;
  - it gives no physical protection.

## Choices made in the animation

**One rigid, clear, flat lens-array panel throughout.**
- Size: about 1.15 m tall and 0.72 m wide, with two upright grips on the back. This sits between the reported Full
  Size and Megashield, so a standing adult can be covered from above the head to the knees.
- It is not a fabric cloak, and it never changes shape.
- The lower legs and shoes stay visible below its bottom edge whenever it is raised. That honestly shows the panel's
  physical boundary.

**The optical effect is confined to the panel's outline.** Inside it, the same wall is redrawn with a strong
horizontal blur:
- horizontal joints and painted bands survive;
- vertical joints smear out.

The person's colours are spread sideways as a faint residual haze, so the illusion is never perfect. The panel's
edges, bevel highlight and surface ridges remain visible.

**One frontal observer position.** The camera is the observer.

**Mechanism diagram.** It is labelled "TOP VIEW · SIMPLIFIED LIGHT PATHS" and uses a few representative rays. It is
not a ray simulation:
- most body light fans out sideways, and a little still reaches the observer (the residual);
- background light from beyond each side is bent across toward the observer.

**What the film does not show or imply:**
- electrical activation, a hologram, a force field or teleportation;
- a transparent body;
- invisibility from every angle;
- defeating thermal cameras or touch;
- guaranteed camera evasion.

**The floating hand.** This is a normal arm reaching round the panel's side edge:
- the part outside the panel renders normally;
- the forearm behind the panel is hidden by the same optical smear;
- the shoulder, elbow, wrist and five fingers stay connected in the rig at all times.
