# The Telepathy Flaw — V5 defect log

What was wrong in V4, what was changed, and how each fix was checked. V4 is untouched. Same story, same 28.000 s / 30 fps / 840 frames, same cuts, same captions, **same audio mix** (sha256 `3948cadd…d891d192`, muxed unchanged; the AAC stream in each V5 export is bit-identical to the one in the V4 export, recorded in `exports/EXPORT_METADATA_V5.json`).

## How "checked" is used here
- **Frame review** = frames pulled from the *final exported MP4* (not the source) in bursts at phone size (300 px wide tiles, 2–6 frames apart) plus full-resolution (1080×1920) crops of the critical frames. Burst windows: 0.7–3.3 s, 9.9–13.5 s, 14.1–15.4 s, 15.4–18.1 s, 19.5–21.3 s, 21.3–24.5 s, 25.35–27.4 s, and frame 839 → frame 0 (and a whole-film half-second sheet, `qa/v5/FILM_SHEET_half_second.png`).
- **Lab renders** = single frames of the same composition through chosen cameras (`tools/views.py`), used while fixing.
- **Rig check** (`source/scripts/check_rig5.ts`, 2,356 solved café/exterior frames) is supporting evidence only: bone lengths, elbow bend side, joint step per frame. It does not prove the drawn anatomy; ownership/silhouette/contact/layering were judged on the rendered frames.
- **Nothing was watched at speed and nothing was heard.** No real-time playback or listening was available. Everything below is frame inspection, measurements and source reading. I cannot say how the film plays at 30 fps, and the audio was verified only as byte/stream identity with V4.

## Caption contrast (captioned vs clean frame, `tools/caption_contrast.py`)
Measured on the final captioned file against the clean file, 251 frames in the caption holds (`qa/v5/CAPTION_CONTRAST.json`): glyph fill vs the dark outline that touches it, minimum **7.1:1** (every measured frame; WCAG-style ratio); glyph fill vs the bare picture just outside the caption's shadow, median **3.42:1**, minimum **1.0:1**. The weakest frames against the picture are 2.3–3.3 s (exterior close-ups, caption over his cream/yellow striped tee and the pale shopfront) and 27.3 s (last close-up). There the caption is readable only because of its outline and shadow (11.3:1 against the outline), not because of the picture behind it. I did not move or restyle the captions beyond the stroke/shadow increase (wording, font, rhythm and timing are locked).

## Layout (caption-lane screening, `check_layout5.ts`, estimated boxes, not pixel measurements)
No face, hand or implant box under the caption lane in any shot except the final push-in: 4 frames with a face box in the lane (V4: 3). The sender's rejection close-up (V4: 42 hand-hit frames) now has 0.

## Residuals and honest limits
- **Not watched, not heard.** Frame inspection only (see above). A playback pass by a person is still needed for timing feel, audio sync feel and any motion that is invisible in stills.
- **Recoil far arm** (26.3–26.7 s): the far forearm emerges from the chest front under the raised near arm. It is attached to the far shoulder (the shoulder crown is visible beside the neck) and the two forearms do not share an elbow, but it is the least clean arm pose in the film.
- **Caption lane:** in several close-ups the caption sits over a forearm or the torso (not hands, cups or faces).
- **Camera tiles:** the lab sheets overlap neighbouring tiles at their edges; those cut fragments are tile edges, not artefacts in the film.
- **Unchanged by instruction:** the line "Humans have thousands of dark, unwanted thoughts every single day" is still unsupported and the render is not fact-approved; the script and the sound were not reopened. Arms stay long and tube-like (Death Clock construction); the standing pair outside is the least refined staging.
- **Rig checks are supporting evidence**; they say nothing about silhouettes or layering.
