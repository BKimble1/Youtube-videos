# The Telepathy Flaw — V3 FINAL (focused cleanup of V2)

V2 is the baseline and its structure is kept: the internal signal journey, the red anomaly, the receiver's reaction, caption style, the C1 narration (the V3 narration stem is bit-identical to V2's) and 28.000 s / 840 frames. V3 lives in `source/src/v3/` (compositions `TelepathyFlawV3`, `TelepathyFlawV3Clean`); V1/V2 files are untouched.

## Fixes completed
1. **Cancellation group.** The whole group (button, ring, dotted track, gate bar, the small packet copy on the track, press/hit state) is now one alpha, `cancelGroup(t)`, driven by one story-state timeline `phaseAt(t)` (`success → captured → leaving → cancel → transit → delivered`). It exists only during the attempt (19.8–21.7 s, the sender visibly reaches and taps; ring spent, red packet already past the gate) and the late attempt (27.02–27.4 s). It is not drawn in the opening or in any successful-message scene. `scripts/audit_layers3.ts` checks every frame against the windows where each group may be visible and fails the build otherwise (`qa/v3/LAYER_AUDIT.json`: pass; the cancel group is visible only in frames 595–650 and 808–821; frames 0 and 839 carry no cancel, warning, card or packet layer).
2. **One implant.** A single circular, low-profile housing in every view: the exterior is a flush disc (skin pad, rim ring, glass lens, two rim lights, contact shadow); the crown window and the macro show the same puck in section, seated in a skull recess with a thin electronics strip, a short decoder channel with three gates, a slim stem and the fine threads into the motor-cortex strip. The dashboard bars, capacitors, LEDs, traces and the static waveform are gone; blue for normal activity, red for the fictional fault. The loop uses the same component at both ends.
3. **The red packet advances.** One red packet, one spline for its progress (no stop-and-go), moving in every view between 15 and 20 s. The repeated face close-up is cut from 1.3 s to 0.65 s and the repeated tracking view is replaced by a 1.15 s insert (17.25–18.4 s) that follows the packet along the route, then a medium view of the sender looking after it and starting the reach. The thread/decoder passage of the red impulse stays where it already was (13.1–15.0 s): repeating it at 17 s would have made a second red packet, so the insert follows the single packet instead. `max_packets_on_screen` is 1.
4. **Payoff and loop kept.** Arrival → recognition → withdrawal and the "Message Delivered" reveal are unchanged. The return still cuts to the lens at 27.4 s and pulls out into frame 0. Lens breathing has a 0.7 s period (divides 28 s), so the light phase continues across the wrap; the camera and aperture both come to rest at 28.0 s and leave rest at 0 s. Picture step at the seam 0.42 (neighbouring steps 0.8–1.5), last vs first frame mean abs diff 0.79; audio step 0.03 vs 0.30 elsewhere.
