V6 corrects how the two characters are posed and how they act. V4 and V5 are untouched. Same story, 28.000 s / 30 fps / 840 frames, same cuts, same scenery, same caption wording/font/rhythm/timing, same mechanism inserts, and **the same audio mix** (sha256 `3948cadd…d891d192`, muxed unchanged; every V6 export's AAC stream is bit-identical to the V4 export's, recorded in `exports/EXPORT_METADATA_V6.json`). ## What "checked" means here
- **Frame review** = frames pulled from the *final exported MP4* (not the source): bursts at phone size (300 px tiles) plus full-resolution crops, over the windows 0.7–3.3 s (greeting), 9.9–13.5 s (both rigs, sip), 14.1–15.4 s (intrusive image), 15.4–18.1 s (rejection, grip, release), 19.5–21.3 s (lap hands), 25.35–27.4 s (receipt, recoil) and the loop seam; plus a whole-film half-second sheet (`qa/v6/FILM_SHEET_half_second.png`) and a pose/contact sheet of the seven requested moments against V4, V5 and the Death Clock reference (`qa/v6/POSE_CONTACT_SHEET.png`).
- **One caveat on timing of the reviews.** The café bursts (sip, rejection, lap, receipt) were reviewed on the V6 render made just before the last two owner-requested changes (the label removal and the exterior two-handed hold). Those two changes touch only the opening exterior scene, its two close-up cameras and the caption layer; the café code is unchanged. I compared the café half of the final file to that render: PSNR 66 dB average, 50 dB minimum over 9.9–28 s (encoder noise only). The exterior was re-reviewed on the final file.
- **Rig and layout checks are supporting evidence only** (`source/scripts/check_rig6.ts`, `check_layout6.ts`); they do not judge silhouettes, contact or emotion.
- **Nothing was watched at speed and nothing was heard.** No real-time playback or listening was available. Everything is frame inspection, measurements and source reading.

## Residuals and honest limits
- **Not watched, not heard.** Frame inspection only. A playback pass by a person is still needed for how the new acting feels at speed.
- **Hands and palms.** The hand drawing is the crew hand (unchanged); palm-on-thigh and fingers-on-cup contact were judged on frames at phone size and full resolution, with no numeric contact measurement.
- **Two-handed hold** is a stacked pair of hands seen from the side (near hand lower, far hand above and behind the cup). It reads as two hands on the cup, but it is a stylised view-specific arrangement, not a full 3D grip.
- **Recoil** (26.3–26.7 s): her far forearm still leaves the front of her torso under the raised near arm; it is attached to the far shoulder and reads as the far arm, but it is the least clean arm pose in the film.
- **Unchanged by instruction:** the line "Humans have thousands of dark, unwanted thoughts every single day" is still unsupported; the script and the sound were not reopened. Arms are still long and tube-like (Death Clock construction). The brain and throat inserts are cartoon diagrams.
