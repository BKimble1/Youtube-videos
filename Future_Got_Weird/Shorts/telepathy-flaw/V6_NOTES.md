# The Telepathy Flaw — V6 (natural character poses)

V4 and V5 are kept untouched (`source/src/v4/`, `source/src/v5/`, their exports and QA). V6 is a copy of V5 in `source/src/v6/` (compositions `TelepathyFlawV6`, `TelepathyFlawV6Clean`, lab `V6Lab`) with the people re-posed and re-acted. It is a correction to the characters only: the 28.000 s / 30 fps / 840-frame edit, the cut list, the story, the scenery, the captions (wording, font, rhythm, timing), the mechanism inserts (V5's round decoder), the imagined fall, the receipt and the loop are V5's, and the audio is the approved V4 mix, byte for byte (`audio/v4/mix/FGW_TelepathyFlaw_V4_mix_48k.wav`, sha256 `3948cadd…d891d192`; `tools/export_v6.py` refuses to run if it changes and checks that each export's AAC stream is bit-identical to the V4 export's). No audio was generated, retimed or remixed.

## What made V5 look like zombies (found in the source, not only the frames)
- **Exterior.** V5 solved the cup hand and the free hand as world targets *forward of the body* (`hand = shoulder + 112 …`, then the far hand at `+30/+40` of it, then `±78` rig units forward) so both people stood with both forearms reaching forward and the wrists hanging. Both stood in the same bent-knee crouch (`hipH 512`, soft-IK 0.96 on the legs, feet at mirrored offsets), and the pair was mirrored.
- **Seated.** The free hand was a *wrist* target 176 units out from the hip (`W_LAP = W_HIP + 176`), past the knee at the table edge, with the hand angle set along the thigh: the hand hovered over the knee, palm toward the viewer.
- **Shoulders.** To make the far arm visible V5 pushed the far shoulder across the chest (`farShoulderV -72/-76` seated, `-74` standing), which lifted the far sleeve into a lobe beside the neck and left the body square to the camera.
- **Face.** The sender's disturbed states (`lid` 0.04–0.05 with `browDown` 0.4, `wide` 0.86–0.88, `open` 0.08–0.14) held half-lids and a slack mouth for seconds.
Passing IK length checks said nothing about any of this.

## What V6 does

### Poses first (`world/exterior.tsx`, `world/interior.tsx`)
- **Standing** (`W_STAND`, `M_STAND`). Authored as silhouettes: weight on one straight leg (the leg soft-IK is eased only at 0.992, `SOFT_LEG`), the other foot ahead with the heel lifted, a pelvis tilt of ±2.5° (opposite for the two), the standing hip height lowered a hair (509) so the support leg is straight but not stretched. She stands with the far foot ahead; he with the near foot ahead. The cup arm is bent with the elbow by the ribs and the cup at the lower chest (cup hand 104/100 rig units forward and 218/222 down from the shoulder); the free arm hangs *behind* the hip (95 / 78 rig units behind the shoulder line, 372 / 364 down) with a neutral wrist and softly curled fingers (two different resting hand shapes). After the first V6 render the owner pointed out, on two screenshots, that the free forearm crossed the front of the shirt and began at the cup arm's elbow, so it read as a third arm. With the hand placed behind the body the whole free arm is behind the torso (its forearm is drawn outside the torso silhouette only), and only the hand and wrist show below the hem: one free arm per figure, a clearly separate silhouette from the cup arm. The body is turned a little toward the partner (new `bodyW` factor: 0.86 outside, 0.84 inside narrows the torso, pelvis and shoulders about the spine line) instead of a frontal mannequin torso.
- **Seated** (`W_LAP`, `M_LAP`, `M_NEAR_LAP`). The free hand now lies *on* its thigh: wrist about 112 units from the hip (his near hand at 34), palm down against the leg, fingers curved over the thigh toward the knee, at the thigh's own angle (12° / 168°), the two hands staggered so each reads as its own forearm. The hovering palms between knees and table are gone. The cup arm is comfortably bent from a lowered shoulder.
- **Shoulders and occlusion** (`rig/Man.tsx`, `rig/Woman.tsx`). V5's far-shoulder offsets are removed (the rig's own `-54/-50` is back); the far shoulder joint sits lower (34 below the shoulder line, was 14) and the far sleeve's crown and the far arm's skin start 30 units down the arm, inside the shirt line, so no sleeve lobe rises beside the neck. The far forearm and hand are still drawn outside the torso only (V5's mask), so they come out of the body's front contour on a readable path, and the far upper arm is allowed to be hidden behind the torso. No hand angle is forced through a changing forearm: the resting hands use the thigh angle only while they rest.
- **Shapes** that did not exist in V5: `ManPose.bw` / `Fig.bodyW` (body width), `Fig.pelvis` (pelvis tilt), per-figure standing parameters (`StandPose`).

### Acting
- **Channels are keyed where the action needs them** (`W_CK`, `M_CK`): head, body, near hand, far hand and cup have their own key tables; V5's fixed per-channel lags (-0.08 … +0.12 s for every action) are gone. The order inside a beat comes from where the keys sit: eyes (expression keys) first, then the head, then the body, then the hands.
- **Cup action.** Look down at the cup (`wCup` gaze, the head dips), lift with the connected grip, the rim reaches the lips (V5's rigid cup and `mouthPoint`), she looks at him over the rim (`wSip`), return, settle on the saucer (cup channel reaches 0 at 12.5 s) and only then the fingers loosen (near-hand grip 0.65 → 0.4 at 12.85 s). Same for the second sip.
- **Before the thought** the pair look comfortable and interested: directed gaze, small gaze changes (he glances at his own cup while she drinks, she looks at the window while he is quiet and back at him), varied smiles (`mEasy`, `mGlow`, `mGlance`, `wWarm`, `wGlad`, `wCup`, `wAside`), one small head tilt per beat, no continuous sway. Normal blinks run outside the gated windows (24.0–24.8 s, 25.2–27.0 s).
- **The thought.** Focus shifts inward (13.0 s), the smile goes, the brows tighten, eyes wide and open (`mFalter` → `mFreeze` → `mDisturb`); after the imagined image he rejects it with the existing short head shake (same timing, a slightly narrower swing) with the eyes squeezed shut only at the peak of the shake (15.85–16.2 s) and opened again quickly (16.3 s); then he holds it in with the eyes open and fixed and the jaw set (`mStrain`, `mHold`: `lid` ≤ 0.03, brows tight, mouth almost closed with teeth showing), not half-lidded or slack.
- **Her fear starts only when the alert reaches her** (`wRead` 25.46 s, `wRecog` 25.78 s, `wAlarm` 26.42 s); before that her faces are `wWarm`/`wCalm`/`wAside`, never wide-eyed.
- **Lap restraint** (20.45–21.3 s). His near hand lies on his near thigh and the far hand on the far thigh (staggered, different angles); they are placed once, stay put while the shoulders come up (`torso` 3, `chest` 2) and a small grip increase, then loosen. No tremor, no fidgeting.
- **Recoil** (25.66–27.4 s): recognition (eyes up first, then the head) → the torso goes back and the chair slides with her (feet stay planted; the chair offset is chair-relative) → the near hand lets go of the cup and comes up on an arc to chest height, the far hand leaves the thigh a beat later on a lower arc nearer her → the body settles. Neither free hand hovers before that.
- **The sender's end gesture** is a small elbow-bent open hand at his chest (it was an outstretched reach), the other hand stays on his thigh.
