# The Telepathy Flaw — V4 (visual rebuild)

V3 is kept untouched (`source/src/v3/`, `exports/..._V3_FINAL_...`). V4 is a new implementation in `source/src/v4/` (compositions `TelepathyFlawV4` and `TelepathyFlawV4Clean`, plus the review lab `V4Lab`). It follows `FGW_Telepathy_Flaw_V3_Review_and_V4_Rebuild_Prompt.md`: the figures, the staging and the message sequence were rebuilt; the approved narration and music are carried over unchanged. 28.000 s, 840 frames, 30 fps.

## What was rebuilt

**Characters (full bodies, Death Clock construction).** The sender is the Death Clock "guesser" rig itself (`rig/Man.tsx`, copied from the Death Clock source: the same skeleton, proportions, ink contour, shading, striped tee, trousers, shoes), with the device code removed and a hand-angle override added. The receiver (`rig/Woman.tsx`) is built on the same skeleton and primitives (same segment lengths, same limb and hand construction), mirrored to face screen-right: grey bob, round glasses, cream shirt, coral cardigan with a finite hem at the hip, trousers, boots. Both have a waist, pelvis, two legs and feet, and sit on real chairs (`env/Props.tsx`). There are no bust tubes any more (`Figures.tsx`, `v3/art/Profile.tsx` are no longer used).
- **Arms are solved every frame.** Hand targets are animated in world space; each arm is a two-bone chain with fixed segment lengths and a fixed elbow side. Nothing blends elbow positions between two IK branches. `scripts/check_rig4.ts` checks every frame of every café shot: segment lengths stay at 1.000 of the rig length (max deviation 0.006 %), the elbow keeps one bend direction per arm (`qa/v4/RIG_CHECK.json`).
- **Heads have a real yaw** (`rig/Head4.tsx`): eyes, brows, nose, lips, cheeks and ears sit on one 3-D face and are projected for the chosen yaw (0° front, ~20–38° three-quarter, 90° profile); the far eye narrows and goes behind the nose bridge, a true profile hides it. Hair is a 3-D shell (domed crown with six spikes / bob with a swept fringe) cut by a hairline and split into back and front layers. The film uses front-to-three-quarter views; the profile is in the pose sheet only.
- **Acting** is carried by brows, lids, pupils, mouth, gaze, posture and timing (`world/interior.tsx`). The receiver's recoil moves her weight (torso back 12°), the chair slides, her hands come up; no figure is scaled. Cups have no handle: the hand wraps the cup body, the thumb on one side and the fingers on the other, and the fist (lap) is a third hand pose.
- Internal pose sheet and Death Clock comparison: `qa/v4/POSE_SHEET.png` (neutral / seated / reaching with the cup / recoiling for both characters, front / three-quarter / profile heads, Death Clock reference frames). Cross-version comparison: `qa/v4/COMPARISON_SHEET.png` (Death Clock vs V3 vs V4: proportions and scene variety).

**One encounter, new places.** A small corner café exterior (sign, striped awning, window with the warm interior, door, planter, chalkboard; `env/Exterior.tsx`) then the café interior (back bar with espresso machine and pastry case, shelving with cups, chalkboard menu, pendant lamps, window as key light, one bistro table with two cups, two chairs; `env/Interior.tsx`). The inserts are separate environments, each with its own look: macro implant in the scalp, a throat cutaway, the glottis, the implant seen through the skull, a neuron and an electrode with out-of-focus foreground and background cells, the brain in profile with the motor strip lit, the chip's interior (a round casing with the implant's blue bezel and rivets), a red variant of the neuron, a field of thousands of dim maroon impulses with a soft scan band, a mechanism diagram.

**Intrusive thought and message.** The red speech-bubble journey, dotted arc, cancel X, packet chase, avatar silhouette, late "Hey", warning card, route and delivery card are gone. Every message now has a visible cause: a thin ring leaves his implant (≈0.4 s) and only then does a small bubble pop beside her head ("Hey" at 2.02 s outside, "Nice place!" at 10.5 s inside) and fade in place; nothing travels. The sender's thought is one clear subjective image: a dark-maroon surround with a scalloped thought bubble and three trailing circles, red-tinted, 0.65 s (20 frames): her chair tips, her cup falls and breaks on the floor, no impact and no injury; her face is alarmed in the image only. A hard cut returns to reality (she is sipping, he is shaken), then he shuts his eyes and shakes it off, grips and lets go of the cup, and, in a separate shot, his hands clench in his lap and stay put ("never intend to act on"). A small "UNFILTERED THOUGHT DETECTED" appears only inside the red neuron insert.

**Ending.** A ring leaves his implant (8 frames); the cut lands on her, still smiling and unaware; a small red alert (a tipping chair and a falling cup, the same picture he had) pops beside her head at 25.38 s; she reads it, jolts back (25.66–25.8 s), freezes; "Message Delivered" confirms it beside her reaction (25.76 s); in the wide that follows she draws back (the chair slides, her hands come up) while the alert and the tag stay attached to her head; then his face as it lands (eyes down, lids open), and the camera falls into his implant and cuts to the macro at the same centre and radius as frame 0. About 2 s of continuous acting. No progress bar, avatar card, lens flare or slow icon.

**Loop.** The closing macro (27.4–28.0 s) pulls back to exactly the opening composition (same centre, same scale, same uneasy tint, no ring in flight) and the opening relaxes it to the calm blue as "this brain chip" is spoken. No cheerful reset, no wipe, no end card.

## Audio
The approved narration and music stems are copied from V3 byte for byte (`audio/v4/stems/narration.wav` sha256 `b8b42ffb4f36e432d086819b6b7030f2e31cda6bab58f15509e6ba12727bfe9a`, equal to V3's; `qa/v4/AUDIO_QA.json` repeats the check). No narration was regenerated, retimed, retagged or stretched; speech ends at 27.39 s; length 28.000 s. Only the effects stem was rebuilt (`tools/build_audio_v4.py`, same synthesised voices and trim as V3): one sound per visible event, no whoosh on cuts; the arrival chime is placed in the gap after "someone", the delivery chime in the gap after "you", the crackle at "until", the eerie return wipe and closing tick are unchanged. Effects get a sidechained ceiling so narration and music are never touched. Mix: −16.0 LUFS, −1.1 dBTP, stems sum exactly to the mix.

## What was reviewed, and how (nothing was watched in real time or listened to)
Frame and source inspection only: a whole-film contact sheet at ~0.5 s steps, a three-frame sheet of every shot (`tools/shotsheet.py`), close inspection of key frames at full resolution, the withdrawal at every second frame, the last second followed by the first second (`tools/seam_check_v4.py`: strip and a measured wrap), a frame-difference scan for pops (`tools/motion_scan.py`), the per-frame rig check above, a caption-lane screening of faces/hands/implant against the camera (`scripts/check_layout4.ts`, `qa/v4/LAYOUT_CHECK.json`), and measured audio figures. There was no real-time playback, no headphone or phone playback and no listening check; audio figures are measurements. I judged the picture by frames; I cannot say how it plays at speed.

## Known limits
- The line "Humans have thousands of dark, unwanted thoughts every single day" is unchanged and remains unsupported. This render is not fact-approved; the replacement suggestion stays in `HANDOFF_V2.md` / `upload/PACKAGING.md`.
- Arms are long and tube-like, as in Death Clock; hands are the crew hands. In several close-ups a hand or a cup sits behind the caption lane (the screening lists them); no face, eye or implant does.
- The standing pair outside is the least refined staging (a stiff stance). The brain and the throat are cartoon diagrams, not anatomy.
- The 4K file was not re-rendered for V4 unless listed in `exports/`.
