## Boundaries for production

The previous Atlas hand had three digits (S2); **do not depict five becoming four**. A ghost pinky depicts a human expectation. DOF count is not finger count, sensor count, or a universal dexterity score. The taped-finger anecdote is an engineering exercise, not a controlled study proving human pinkies unnecessary. The three-actuator explanation is about adding a finger to this design. No claim that four digits beat all five-digit hands, that human hands are obsolete, that a factory deployment is independently verified, or that Atlas learns without supervision. Reconstructions must remain visibly illustrative. Inspect the real source hand before final prop work.

Script statements are paraphrases; no long source quotation is required on screen. Avoid turning the title into a claim that an existing five-digit Atlas was mutilated or downgraded.
