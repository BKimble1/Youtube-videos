# Handoff QA and production status

This file describes the handoff itself. It is not the final video's QA report.

## Performed in this preparation session

- Read the user's brief and the available native cast/toolchain reference files.
- Inspected the approved counter style frame; existing cast recovery/inspection is documented in provenance.
- Checked the company design source and direct IEEE interview, including the previous hand's digit count.
- Compared a new story with existing episode concepts and read current published YouTube audience/analytics research.
- Wrote a complete narration, beat plan, transitions, sound cues, production workflow, factual ledger, and measurement plan.
- Created and visually inspected a schematic storyboard.
- Checked structured beat continuity, narration paragraph mapping, JSON/CSV validity, package integrity, and approved-asset byte identity.
- Verified that the packet contains no rendered-film claim, no invented final alignment, and no credential files.

## Not performed here

- No ElevenLabs generation, account/model availability check, or audition.
- No Runway generation or paid production call.
- No final Remotion source/timeline build, compile, or film render.
- No real narration alignment, final subtitle timing, loudness measurement, or audiovisual film review.
- No live YouTube Analytics account pull, Google Trends volume measurement, or verified topic-specific Shorts view-count dataset.
- No repository mutation, commit, push, YouTube upload, or publication in this preparation task.

The master prompt instructs Claude to perform the outstanding production/QA work autonomously when executed with the necessary tools. It cannot confer an unavailable tool, subscription entitlement, or model selector. If the production environment has a genuine missing capability, it must return concrete completed outputs and identify the remaining step accurately.
