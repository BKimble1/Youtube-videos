## Boundaries honoured in the picture

- No five-digit previous Atlas hand and no "5 → 4" counter anywhere. The pinky position is only a dashed **ghost** outline labelled by the circle ("human expectation").
- The robot hand is a **simplified original reconstruction** (thumb + three fingers); no company photo, frame or footage was used.
- "13 ways to move" is a count of controllable motion, drawn as **13 identical dots**: no per-finger breakdown is implied, and the dots are not a joint blueprint.
- B02 is an **illustrative reconstruction** of the taped-finger exercise using a human-hand prop; the mug and knob are illustrations, not measured tasks. The checker is a reenacting guide, not a portrait of an engineer.
- B05 shows **company-reported abilities as illustrations**, not demonstration footage. B07 is a design-goal illustration, not a deployment claim.
- No dollar figures, failure percentages, benchmarks or quotes appear. The price-tag prop is blank.
- Capabilities and the experiment are attributed to the company in narration ("Boston Dynamics says…", "The company says…"); the chip repeats the source on every beat.
