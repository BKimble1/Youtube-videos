## What this audit does **not** establish

Nobody has watched or listened to V4. The retimed (1.05×) narration, the new effects and the new bed are unauditioned by ear; whether the retimed pace feels rushed on a phone is open — the original-pace cut is delivered for that reason. No promise about views or retention is made or implied.
