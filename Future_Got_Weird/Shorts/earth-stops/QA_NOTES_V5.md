## What the numbers do not say
Frame differences, contact distances, loudness and speech-to-effects ratios are supporting checks. They cannot show that the foot is noticed, that the stumble feels like lost footing, that the second person feels like a consequence rather than a gag, that a ducked crash still feels like a hit, or that the film will hold attention. They are not an audience test and not a performance prediction.
