## Ending and loop
The film ends on a calm hold (the coffee payoff) at f759; there is **no end screen, no subscribe interruption and no pull-back**. The loop is a **hard cut to the action frame** (the last frame is not the first frame); a concise resolved ending was chosen over a slow pull-back because an exact loop would have cost seconds after the payoff.

## Draft pinned description (**do not publish** before the source pages are opened by a person)
> A hypothetical: if Earth's solid ground stopped turning suddenly, loose things, the air and the sea would at first keep moving east at the speed the ground had. At the equator that is about 1,040 mph; it falls toward the poles with the cosine of latitude. The stop is imaginary; the film doesn't claim how it could happen or what it would do. Sources (read by the video's reviewer, not by the author): NASA Cosmicopia Earth/Moon FAQ, NGA WGS 84, NASA/JPL planetary parameters, NASA Glenn on inertia. *(URLs to be added after a person opens them.)*
