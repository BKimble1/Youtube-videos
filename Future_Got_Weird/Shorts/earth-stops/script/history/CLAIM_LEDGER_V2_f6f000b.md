## Read this first
1. **The imaginary event is kept apart from the real physics.** The stipulation (S1, S7a) stops the *solid Earth* abruptly. It does **not** stop people, loose objects, air or water, and the film invents no mechanism for how Earth stops. Everything after the stipulation is ordinary inertia: gravity and contact forces still act; nothing is predicted.
2. **Who read what.** Claude (the author of this cut) **did not open any source page** (every source host was refused by the sandbox: `curl` returned 000 on all six). The reads marked R1–R6 were made by the reviewer (ChatGPT) and are recorded as the reviewer's, with the access note the reviewer gave. Nothing is marked "confirmed" on Claude's say-so. Galileo, IERS and the other carried-over originals (U1–U4) are **uninspected** and are not marked confirmed.
3. Statuses: **STIPULATED** = the premise; **COMPUTED** = derived from a primary constant by `tools/calc_speeds.py` (reproducible); **REVIEWER-READ** = the reviewer opened the page and reports it supports the claim (Claude did not); **EVERYDAY** = an ordinary statement, not a claim about instruments; **UNINSPECTED** = a source nobody has opened.
