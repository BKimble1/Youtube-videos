# Claim ledger — Earth Stops. You Don't. (V1, as built)

Covers every spoken or on-screen claim in the **V1 review cut** (`SCRIPT.md`, `exports/v4/`). Sources: `SOURCES.md` (S1–S10). Numbers: `speed_table.json`, reproducible with `tools/calc_speeds.py`. Supersedes the H1-era ledger (hook only / proposal script); changes are listed under "What changed in V1".

## Read this first
1. **No source page was opened** (network policy; see `SOURCES.md`). "Excerpt only" means a web-search excerpt that quotes the source. Nothing in this ledger is "confirmed against the page".
2. **The hypothetical is explicit.** The first words are "What if", a `WHAT IF` tag is on screen from the first frame until "None of this can really happen" has been said and the globe has turned, and comes back for the loop; S7 says "None of this can really happen." No claim here says Earth could stop, or that the depicted outcome is what a real stop would look like.
3. Statuses: **COMPUTED** = derived from a primary constant (reproducible); **EXCERPT** = a search excerpt of a primary source; **SECONDARY** = only an explainer reports it; **ANALOGY** = a teaching device, not a physical claim; **STIPULATED** = the premise of the video.

## What changed in V1 (against the H1-era ledger)
- **C04 (bus) is gone.** The bus analogy was removed by the owner. C04 is now the plain statement "Stop the ground, and anything that isn't fixed to it keeps going", shown in a plaza (loose things leave, fixed things stay).
- **C05 (air and ocean) is kept and built.** Picture: wind, then sea surge. Both are **not to scale** (V9).
- **C06/C07/C13 chart values are the computed ones**: 1,040 / 798 / **181** mph (the proposal had rounded 800 and 180). The bars are proportional to these.
- **C09/C10 wording as spoken:** "…her coffee's been moving about a thousand miles an hour all along" (not "doing").
- **Open item 2 (plate without the tilde) is resolved:** the speed plate reads `~1,000`.
- New on-screen strings are in the table below (`NO SLOSH`, `ALL MOVING EAST — TOGETHER`, `FIXED`, `LOOSE →`, `AT FIRST`, `STARTING SPEED ONLY`, `157 MPH = CATEGORY 5`).

## Frame, in one place
All speeds are those of the ground relative to Earth's rotation axis, as seen from space, in the instant before the hypothetical stop. They fall with the cosine of latitude (C06). On a rotating Earth the equator's centripetal acceleration (0.35 % of g) is why weight is slightly lower there; the film does not mention it.
