# Earth Stops. You Don't. — script (V4, as built)

**Status: V4 review cut, 20.5 s, built and exported (`exports/v7/`). Not published, not uploaded, not scheduled.** This is the script as it is in the film. V3 (24.8 s, `243eb4d`), V2 (25.33 s, `f6f000b`) and V1 (34.5 s, `701648d`) are preserved: `script/history/SCRIPT_V3_243eb4d.md`, `SCRIPT_V2_f6f000b.md`, `SCRIPT_V1_701648d.md`.

V4 is the owner's single finishing pass on V3: the same concept (an absurd readable trigger → immediate personal danger → loose objects, wind, ocean → one concise explanation → one globe comparison → a loop that returns to frame 0), with the narration re-spoken so that **there are no long pauses between phrases**, the middle two lines rewritten to name what the air and the ocean do, and every shot animated continuously. **The stop is an impossible visual metaphor: nothing in the film claims a brake, or any way of stopping Earth, is feasible; the brake arrests the spin, not the orbit.**

Narration: ElevenLabs **Test Voice** (`kk5XaSLo2XAw0sKM98zU`, `eleven_v4`, account defaults, no tags), **one generation of the whole text** (2 variations; **take A** used, 21.76 s). The take's own between-phrase pauses were shortened by removing audio from the **middle of each silent gap** (`tools/build_voice_v4.py`; 12 gaps, 1.44 s removed in total, 6 ms equal-power joins inside the silence); each remaining gap is 0.10–0.45 s (0.22, 0.24, 0.10, 0.28, 0.26, 0.24, 0.28, **0.45**, 0.32, 0.21, 0.30, 0.30 s — the 0.45 s one, after *coast.*, is the only deliberate beat: the wave's aftermath registers before the explanation begins). **Nothing inside a word was cut, stretched, sped up or pitch-shifted** (measured: each kept piece equals its take-A slice at −70 dB residual or better). Scribe returned the sent text word-for-word (64 words). **Nobody has listened to the narration.**

## What changed from V3 (`script/history/SCRIPT_V3_243eb4d.md`)
- **No long pauses.** V3 had silent gaps of 1.0–3.5 s between lines (the ocean beat, the film's "wave"); V4's longest gap is 0.45 s. The picture is timed to the actual words.
- **Words**: 64 (V3 55). *So does the air.* → *So does the air, driving debris through the streets.*; *And the ocean.* → *And the ocean keeps moving into the coast.* Both added lines say what the picture shows (debris carried by air; ocean water continuing into a shore). Everything else is the V3 text, verbatim.
- **The first speed plate carries `EQUATOR` from its first appearance** (V3 added the label later): `EQUATOR ~1,000 MPH →`.
- **Personal danger is acted, not frozen**: the 5-frame suspended beat is gone; the first second is 0.93 s of continuous acting (stumble, feet leave the ground, fear grows, coffee leaves the cup).
- **The brake is simplified**: a plain belt (no slots/ticks), twice-as-large pads, a visible lock reaction (pads dig in, bracket shudders, sparks, brief flash), no shock ring; the lock lands inside *stopped*, on its /t/ closure, with a short high-passed clack so the word stays intelligible.
- **The coast was rebuilt**: one water-surface source of truth, the boat floats on it and is carried up its front face, capsizes at the lip; the pier visibly changes (post snaps, deck section detaches, one post tilts); the transition is carried by the flood and drain, not a detached band.
- **The ending begins during the final line** (the caliper returns from 18.07 s) and has no tail; the film restarts as soon as the last word is over.
