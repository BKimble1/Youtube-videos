## Runway decision: **HOLD**
Brief: "Hold Runway until the storyboard identifies a shot where it provides a clear benefit."
- Every shot above is feasible in Remotion with existing parts (globe, street, character rig, bicycle, tags). Nothing needs photoreal imagery or fluid simulation.
- The **only** candidate is **B04** (sea and air sliding past a fixed pier: water and foliage motion are what vector animation draws least convincingly). It is also **optional** (line S5 can be cut), and Runway output would have to match the flat vector style and the anonymous original cast, which is a style-risk of its own.
- **Decision:** build B04 in Remotion first. Only if the owner sees it and calls it flat do I ask for a Runway test, naming one 5 s shot, the prompt, the style references and the credit cost **before** any generation. No Runway tool was called in this session (no generation and no credit or plan lookup).

## Music: **HOLD.** No music has been generated (Eleven Music not run). The sound plan relies on physical sound and silence; a music bed would need the owner's approved source.

## Draft pinned description (**do not publish** before the source pages are opened)
> A hypothetical: if Earth stopped turning instantly, everything not fixed to the ground would keep moving east at the speed the ground had. At the equator that's about 1,040 mph (465 m/s); it falls with the cosine of latitude (about 180 mph at 80° N, above the 157 mph Category 5 threshold). It can't actually happen, and the film doesn't claim what a real stop would do. Speeds are relative to Earth's axis. Sources: WGS 84 (NGA); NASA Earth Fact Sheet; NOAA NHC Saffir-Simpson Hurricane Wind Scale; Galileo, *Dialogue Concerning the Two Chief World Systems* (1632); Newton, *Principia*; NASA Cosmicopia; Smithsonian. *(URLs to be added once the pages have been opened; none has been opened yet.)*
