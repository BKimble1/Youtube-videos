## Loop (seam f743 → f0)
Camera, globe centre/radius/orientation, rotation rate (4°/frame), background gradient/stars, `WHAT IF` tag, and the pad gap (`padGap(t)`, one function for both sides) are shared constants. Measured on the encode: the f743→f0 frame difference is the same size as any ordinary consecutive-frame difference except for the caption that appears at f0 (`qa/v6/QA_V3.json`). The audio is continuous by construction (periodic hum; strain split at t = 0; first/last sample differ by 0.0002 of full scale).
