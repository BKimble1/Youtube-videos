# Earth Stops. You Don't. — script (V5, as built)

**Status: V5 review cut, 20.5 s, built and exported (`exports/v8/`). Not published, not uploaded, not scheduled.** This is the script as it is in the film. **The words, the voice take, the pauses and the captions are exactly V4's** (the V5 voice-only mp3 is byte-identical to V4's); V5 changes the picture of two beats and the mix under three words. V4 (20.5 s, `2fb391a`), V3 (24.8 s, `243eb4d`), V2 (25.33 s, `f6f000b`) and V1 (34.5 s, `701648d`) are preserved: `script/history/SCRIPT_V4_2fb391a.md`, `SCRIPT_V3_243eb4d.md`, `SCRIPT_V2_f6f000b.md`, `SCRIPT_V1_701648d.md`.

V4 was the owner's single finishing pass on V3 (V5 is a further, narrower one; see "What changed from V4"): the same concept (an absurd readable trigger → immediate personal danger → loose objects, wind, ocean → one concise explanation → one globe comparison → a loop that returns to frame 0), with the narration re-spoken so that **there are no long pauses between phrases**, the middle two lines rewritten to name what the air and the ocean do, and every shot animated continuously. **The stop is an impossible visual metaphor: nothing in the film claims a brake, or any way of stopping Earth, is feasible; the brake arrests the spin, not the orbit.**

Narration: ElevenLabs **Test Voice** (`kk5XaSLo2XAw0sKM98zU`, `eleven_v4`, account defaults, no tags), **one generation of the whole text** (2 variations; **take A** used, 21.76 s). The take's own between-phrase pauses were shortened by removing audio from the **middle of each silent gap** (`tools/build_voice_v4.py`; 12 gaps, 1.44 s removed in total, 6 ms equal-power joins inside the silence); each remaining gap is 0.10–0.45 s (0.22, 0.24, 0.10, 0.28, 0.26, 0.24, 0.28, **0.45**, 0.32, 0.21, 0.30, 0.30 s — the 0.45 s one, after *coast.*, is the only deliberate beat: the wave's aftermath registers before the explanation begins). **Nothing inside a word was cut, stretched, sped up or pitch-shifted** (measured: each kept piece equals its take-A slice at −70 dB residual or better). Scribe returned the sent text word-for-word (64 words). **Nobody has listened to the narration.**

## What changed from V4 (`script/history/SCRIPT_V4_2fb391a.md`)
- **Words, voice take, pauses, captions: unchanged.** No new voice generation; `FGW_EarthStops_V5_VoiceOnly_48k.mp3` has the same sha256 as V4's.
- **The opening shows the loss of footing** (0.67–0.97 s): V4 cut to her already stumbling with the cup already pouring and her lowest foot riding along with her. V5 shows one foot clearly planted on the pavement (pinned in world coordinates as the camera rides east, with a contact shadow and a scuff), the leg compressing as her torso carries on, the cup tipping, the foot dragged off, coffee escaping, both feet clear by 0.97 s, and then a short parabola (no launch, no float). Danger is still inside the first second; the street entry (0.67 s), the lock (0.50 s) and the cut (1.60 s) are unchanged.
- **One more person in the wind shot** (6.57–7.47 s): an adult from the approved cast is carried east in the middle ground, readable at phone size, passing behind the foreground post and in front of the awning, and gone before the awning tears; the opening woman does not reappear.
- **Speech clarity**: local, short reductions of the effects' speech-band and sibilant energy on *stopped*, *debris through the streets* and the final /st/ of *coast.*, with the effects recovering afterwards; retimed footsteps, scuffs and coffee, and two quiet Foley layers for the second person.

## What changed from V3 (`script/history/SCRIPT_V3_243eb4d.md`) — in V4
- **No long pauses.** V3 had silent gaps of 1.0–3.5 s between lines; V4's longest gap is 0.45 s. The picture is timed to the actual words.
- **Words**: 64 (V3 55). *So does the air.* → *So does the air, driving debris through the streets.*; *And the ocean.* → *And the ocean keeps moving into the coast.*
- **The first speed plate carries `EQUATOR` from its first appearance**: `EQUATOR ~1,000 MPH →`.
- **Personal danger acted, not frozen**; **the brake simplified** (plain belt, twice-as-large pads, a lock reaction); **the coast rebuilt** (one water surface, boat lifted and capsized, pier visibly broken, flood and drain transition); **the ending begins during the final line** and has no tail.
