Covers every spoken or on-screen claim in the **V3 review cut** (`SCRIPT.md`, `exports/v6/`). Sources and who read them: `SOURCES.md`. The V2 ledger (`f6f000b`) and V1 ledger (`701648d`) are kept in `history/`; claims that were retired earlier (hurricane comparison, 40°/80° chart, "within minutes", bus, disclaimer scene) stay retired.

## Read this first
1. **The imaginary event is kept apart from the real physics.** The film stipulates that the *solid Earth* is arrested by an oversized brake. The brake is **an impossible visual metaphor**: the film makes no claim that such a brake, or any way of stopping Earth's rotation, is feasible, and the brake arrests the spin, **not the orbit** (nothing is said or shown about Earth's motion around the Sun). The stop does not stop people, loose objects, air or water: after the stipulation everything is ordinary inertia with gravity and contact forces still acting; nothing is predicted.
2. **Who read what.** Claude (author of this cut) **opened no source page** (every host refused or unresolvable). R1–R6 are the reviewer's reads, recorded as the reviewer's. Nothing is marked "confirmed" on Claude's say-so.
3. Statuses: **STIPULATED** = the premise; **COMPUTED** = derived from a primary constant by `tools/calc_speeds.py`; **REVIEWER-READ** = the reviewer opened the page and reports it supports the claim; **EVERYDAY** = an ordinary statement; **UNINSPECTED** = nobody opened it.
4. **The qualifier "initially".** V2 said "initially" in the line; the V3 script (the brief's script) does not contain the word. The qualification is carried by "that **starting** speed" (S6), by the picture (the street shows the first instants only), and here. **Open item 2** asks whether the owner wants "initially" spoken.
