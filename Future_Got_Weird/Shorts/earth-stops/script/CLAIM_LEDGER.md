# Claim ledger — Earth Stops. You Don't.

Covers every spoken or on-screen claim in the proposed script (`SCRIPT.md`) and in the built hook (`exports/v3/`). Sources: `SOURCES.md` (S1–S10). Numbers: `speed_table.json`, reproducible with `tools/calc_speeds.py`.

## Read this first
1. **No source page was opened** (network policy; see `SOURCES.md`). "Excerpt only" below means a web-search excerpt that quotes the source. Nothing in this ledger is "confirmed against the page".
2. **The hypothetical is explicit.** S1 opens with "What if", the `WHAT IF` tag stays on screen through the hook, and S7 says "None of this can really happen" before the callback. No claim here says Earth could stop, or that the depicted outcome is what a real stop would look like.
3. Statuses: **COMPUTED** = derived by me from a primary constant (reproducible); **EXCERPT** = a search excerpt of a primary source; **SECONDARY** = only an explainer reports it; **ANALOGY** = a teaching device, not a physical claim; **STIPULATED** = the premise of the video.

## Frame, in one place
All speeds are those of the ground relative to Earth's rotation axis, as seen from space, in the instant before the hypothetical stop. They fall with the cosine of latitude (C06). On a rotating Earth the equator's centripetal acceleration (0.35 % of g) is why weight is slightly lower there; we do not mention it in the film.

## Open items for the owner
1. **Equator chip.** C02 holds only near the equator. The `EQUATOR` chip is what makes "this street … about a thousand miles an hour" true. Keep it in every cut of the hook.
2. **Plate says `1,000`, speech says "about a thousand", bicycle tag says `~1,000`.** The plate lacks the tilde and the exact figure is 1,040. Options: (a) add `~` to the plate (small re-render of the hook; no audio change), (b) change the plate to a roll that ends on `1,040` (the number would then disagree with the spoken "about a thousand" by design, which some viewers will read as extra precision), (c) leave it. I recommend (a). Not done: the hook is frozen for your review.
3. **"About a thousand" is 3.9 % low.** "Just over a thousand" would be exact for the equator. The voice take says "about a thousand"; changing it costs another narration generation. Recommendation: keep it.
4. **Corollary V needs a non-rotating frame.** The bridge from Galileo/Newton to Earth's circular motion is the computed 0.35 % of g (C03). If you want a stricter line than "you can't feel it", it needs a qualifier we have no room for in the film.
5. **S5 attribution to NASA Cosmicopia** is unverified second-hand text (the search tool itself said so). Replace with the real page text once opened, or cite Smithsonian instead.
6. **The optional cuts** (S4 bus, S5 air/ocean) are each independent of the numeric claims; dropping them removes C04–C05 only.
