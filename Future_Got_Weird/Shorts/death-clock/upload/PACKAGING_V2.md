## Title (plainly hypothetical)
**Preferred:** What If a Watch Could Tell When You're Dying?

**Alternate:** An Imaginary Death Clock: Would You Want to Know?

## Description (ready to paste)

> An imagined future wristband that tells you exactly when you are dying, and calls for help before you black out. Everything in this Short is fiction: the injected nano-sensors, the countdown to the second, the rescue drone, the wrist treatment and the 42-year forecast. No device like this exists, and nothing here is medical advice. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number.
>
> If you had one, would you want to know?
