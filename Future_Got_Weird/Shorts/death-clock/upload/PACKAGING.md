## Title

## Description (ready to paste)

> A wristband that tells you exactly when you're dying, then calls for help before you black out. This is an imagined future device: the injected nano-sensors, the second-by-second countdown, the automatic injection and the 42-year forecast are all fictional. No product like this exists, and nothing here is medical advice. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number.
>
> If you had one, would you want to know?
