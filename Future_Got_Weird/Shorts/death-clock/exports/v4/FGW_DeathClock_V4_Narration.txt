This device tells you exactly when you are dying. And he has just over two minutes to change it.

Under the band, microscopic nano-sensors slip through his skin and race into his bloodstream. They sweep past blood cells, monitoring cellular decay and sending a constant stream of signals.

If an organ starts failing, or his heart slips into a lethal rhythm, the warning races back to his wrist. The red screen keeps counting. Every second matters.

His location and medical data reach a nearby hospital, where an emergency drone launches straight toward him. While help is coming, the band delivers an automatic injection, potentially buying him time.

His heartbeat steadies as he sits up. Green replaces red: life expectancy, forty-two years. But that number already changed once. If your countdown started, would you want to know?
