**Title idea:** They Fired a Laser Into a Thunderstorm

**Description:**

In summer 2021, researchers set up a powerful laser on the Säntis mountain in Switzerland, next to a 124 m
telecommunications tower. It fired about a thousand pulses per second above the tower's tip. During a storm, an
upward lightning discharge from the tower followed the laser's path for about 50 m (over 160 ft) — extending the
tower's lightning rod into the sky with nothing but air.

Animated illustration: the beam, the pulses and the molecular views are explanatory drawings, timing is slowed, and
the danger scene is an imagined example (not part of the experiment).
