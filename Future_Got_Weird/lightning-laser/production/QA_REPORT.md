# Lightning Laser V1: QA report

All checks below were made on the rendered files (`exports/`), not on the source, unless marked "source". Nobody has
watched the film in real time with sound: this environment has no display or speakers. "Playback" here means frame
strips at every frame across each transition, contact sheets, a frame-difference scan of all 1005 frames, the 3× loop
file inspected the same way, and two speech recognisers on the MP4's own audio.

## Defects found in renders and fixed before this version

- arms crossing the face/torso on both characters (pose test sheets; elbows re-solved);
- the shelter caption over her hand on the knob;
- a foreground rock slab blacking out the apparatus close-up;
- the molecular view fading to 40 % during the heating and path beats; the pulse drawn as a hard block;
- the payoff channel nearly invisible; pulse packets turning into bright bars in the close-ups at the seam;
- a near-jump cut between the leader's tip and the wider view (now one continuous pull-back);
- the whole vignette backdrop brightening with the strike (now only a local glow);
- molecule hand-overs happening in about 3 frames (now spread across the zoom in log scale);
- molecules leaking into the loop close-up (broke the seam; now limited to the G–J sequence);
- a soft clipper applied to the whole mix (level −14.5 LUFS); replaced with a peak limiter;
- captions carrying 1–5 frames across four cuts (cuts moved onto the caption changes).

## Remaining problems and limits (not fixed)

- **Not watched in real time with sound.** Timing, pacing and the mix should get one normal-speed viewing.
- The operator is a reconstruction (see `SOURCE_NOTES.md`, Asset limitations); the contacts, laser-voices and Death
  Clock projects are not in the repository, so the lead's green eyes and her design could not be compared with them.
- Research pages could not be fetched here (network policy); facts come from search extracts.
- The zoom into the pulse (11.6–11.95 s) and into the air (6.6–6.85 s) change a lot per frame by nature (fast,
  detailed zooms); no frame pops, but they are the busiest moments.
- The interior's wall is plain; the house shot has no character. Both are deliberate (time goes to the mechanism).
- The 3× loop file (`qa/*_loop_x3.mp4`, about 90 MB) is not committed; `tools/qa.py` regenerates it.
