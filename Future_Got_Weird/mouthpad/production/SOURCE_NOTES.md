## How the device is drawn (and why)

- **Shape:** a clear, custom-fit upper retainer (dental-grade resin) with cups over the upper teeth and a palatal
  plate, about 1 mm thick: never a bulky mouthguard and never a loose chip (search: augmental.tech, MIT Media Lab).
- **Gold pad:** a small square of gold flexible circuit on the palate, just behind the front teeth region, facing the
  tongue (Augmental X post). Drawn about 14.5 × 12.5 mm. **Verify** the exact size and position against a product
  photo.
- **Traces and batteries:** gold serpentine traces run from the pad to battery modules at the rear molars, sealed in
  the resin (Augmental X post). Their exact layout in V1 is illustrative.
- **Head motion sensor:** inside the device (accelerometer and gyroscope, per IEEE Spectrum summary); not drawn as a
  separate part.
- **Not shown on purpose:** sip-to-right-click, scroll and swipe gestures, the companion app, battery life (7+ h),
  weight (about 10 g), price ($1,400 plus a dental scan). None is in the narration.

## Accuracy limits in the animation

- The tongue's touch point on the pad maps to W/A/D in the split screen (screen-left on the pad = strafe left).
  The real mapping is set per user in Augmental's app; the direction convention is a simplification.
- The assistive-use montage shows one person (a Weird Crew cast member in a power wheelchair) moving a cursor with
  small head movements and clicking with the tongue, and answering a phone call. It does not imply any level of
  performance.
- The game is original (Zip, the goo-bots, Rex, the arena); no real game footage or manufacturer footage is used.
