## Description (paste)

Figure trained its retired F.02 humanoid robots to jump into molten steel. Here's why a robotics company would do that, how you teach a robot a jump it can only make once, and what the stunt does and doesn't prove.

The robots weren't choosing anything: they ran a movement policy trained toward a goal people picked. That's the surprising part, and the useful one.
