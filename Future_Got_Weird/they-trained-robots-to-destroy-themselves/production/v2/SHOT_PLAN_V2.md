# Shot plan, V2 ("They Trained Robots to Destroy Themselves")

All times are in seconds on the master timeline, rounded to 0.1 s.

- Picture and sound share one cue system. Every boundary comes from the word timing of the locked V2 narration
  (`source/src/v2/cues.ts`, `T('phrase')`). The sound cue sheet `source/src/v2/sfx_v2.ts` reads the same cues and the
  same motion objects (jump, trial and walk timings). Editing a line therefore moves picture and sound together.
- Runtime: 233.5 s (3:53.5) at 30 fps native, 1920×1080.
- Shot counts:
  - Hard cuts and wipes, measured on the rendered master: see `QA_REPORT.md` (V2 section).
  - Distinct framings planned below: 83, which averages 2.8 s each.

**Legend**

- **Focus**: where the eye should be at the start of the shot, then at its end. The focus at a shot's end is where the
  next shot's focus begins.
- **Cut**: how the shot is entered.
- **Sound**: effects that start in the shot. The score is described per section.
