# Laser Voices V5: shot plan

The narration is locked; its timings are from `data/timeline.json`.

**Direction.** Things happen in the frame and the camera mostly watches. There are 13 shots, joined by clean cuts or
match cuts, and only three deliberate camera moves (M1–M3).

**Screen geography, the same in every world.** The transmitter is on screen right and the listener on screen left,
so the beam always travels right to left:
- the atrium (seen from the front);
- the ear cutaway;
- the molecular air;
- the transmitter cutaway;
- the sweep diagram;
- the lab.

## Staging (the atrium, World 1 and World 4)

- **The lead (the guesser)** stands at an exhibit case: a brass ear trumpet under glass. He faces the front of the
  hall, toward the camera side.
- **His friend (clerkB, with her coffee)** stands on his right.
- **The transmitter** is on a tripod inside a rope-off demonstration stand, about 4.6 m to his left, at ear height,
  set up on his left ear. The beam is horizontal.
- **The third person** (a visitor with a museum map, from the channel's other cast) walks from the back of the hall
  toward the front, straight across the beam line between the transmitter and the lead.
- **The crowd** walks only behind them, so nobody else crosses the beam.

## The three camera moves

**M1: into the ear (S4, 5.55–6.10 s).**
- Before: his head in close-up.
- Reveals: the inside of the ear and the empty canal, which the outside can't show.
- Why not a cut: the registration (the same ear becoming its own cutaway) shows it is his ear.
- Space: the ear stays at the same screen position throughout.
- Narration: "you wouldn't need anything in your ears".

**M2: along the beam into the air (S6, 8.70–9.40 s).**
- Before: the whole setup, with the visualised beam.
- Reveals: where the sound is made, in the air at his ear, at a scale nobody can see.
- Why not a cut: following the beam keeps the "where" (beside his ear, in the beam).
- Space: it is the beam's own path.
- Narration: "water vapor near your ear".

**M3: from the molecules to the ear (S8, 13.45–14.15 s).**
- Before: the pressure bands forming in the air.
- Reveals: their destination, the ear canal and eardrum.
- Why not a cut: it connects the microscopic cause to the anatomical effect in one space.
- Narration: "creating tiny sound waves".

Every other shot is locked. Life comes from the action, the crowd in the background and small idle motion.

## Loop

- The film ends in the S1 composition (S13), with the whisper beginning again and his startle running across the
  seam.
- The lead and friend do not walk at the seam, so nothing is shifted.
- The crowd's lanes advance by whole lane widths over the film, which makes them exactly periodic.
- Every idle motion uses whole cycles over the film.
