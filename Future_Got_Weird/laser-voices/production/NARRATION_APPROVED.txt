If someone pointed this laser at you, you could hear a voice that nobody beside you hears.

And you wouldn’t need anything in your ears.

The beam is invisible. But when water vapor near your ear absorbs its light, it heats up and expands—creating tiny sound waves.

Now, sweeping that beam at exactly the right speed concentrates the sound where you’re standing.

Scientists actually demonstrated this from eight feet away.

But if someone walks between you and the laser…

The voice suddenly stops.

They can’t hear your message.

They’re blocking it.

And the second they move…

You hear it again.
