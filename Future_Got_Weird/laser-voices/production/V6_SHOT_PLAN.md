# Laser Voices V6: shot plan

V6 is the final production pass on V5. The direction, the narration and the mix are V5's; V6 rebuilds the character
rigs, the science and technology shots, and the ending.

**Screen geography (every world).** The transmitter is on screen right and the listener on the left, so the beam
always travels right to left.

**Camera.**
- Shots are locked, with at most a 1–2% focal drift.
- Ideas are joined by clean cuts.
- There are three camera moves, each into a new scale: M1, M2 and M3.

## Transitions

- Clean cuts at 1.5, 3.0, 5.0, 15.0, 17.75, 20.0, 23.37, 27.0, 27.75, 30.3 and 32.3 s.
- Three moves, each into a new scale and each used once (no push-in/pull-out pairs):
  - M1: person → inside the ear;
  - M2: ear → the air molecules;
  - M3: molecules → the eardrum.
- Science → machine is a clean cut, as is machine → lab sweep. The sweep shot and the demonstration share one lab, so
  the cut between them is a change of angle within the same place.
- The hall returns only for the interruption, which is new information.
