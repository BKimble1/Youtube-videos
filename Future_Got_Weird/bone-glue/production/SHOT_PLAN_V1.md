# Bone Glue V1: shot plan and transition list

Times are from the narration's measured word timings (the source keys every beat to `word()`, so they move with the
final alignment). One continuous wrist world (millimetres, the patient's right forearm palm up, hand pointing up the
frame; radius on the thumb side), plus one editorial match out to an oyster and back.

| Shot | Time (s) | Words | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.25 | "If you shattered your wrist," | Frame 0: straight down into the incision; the fractured distal radius fills the frame (bone vertical, crack horizontal). The camera tracks right with the tip; by 1.0 s the bead bridges the crack and fills the gap. |

| 2 | 4.0–5.95 | "Researchers actually did it." | Continues widening into the compact clinical scene: hand table and drape, the surgeon (Weird Crew, cap, mask, gown, light-blue gloves) seated at the right finishing the bead. The applicator lifts; his eyes smile above the mask on "did it". |

| 4 | 10.33–13.6 | "It grips bone even inside a wet, bloody wound." | Macro on the bead across the aligned fragments (the gap is full of gel, its edges keyed into the surface pores); blood-tinted fluid runs over bone and bead; the edges stay sealed. |

Loop: a push back into the wrist through skin, fat and muscle into frame 0's view. |
