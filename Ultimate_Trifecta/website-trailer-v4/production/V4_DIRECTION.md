## 2. Correct the excessive blurriness

This is one of the highest-priority improvements.

Certain shots look appropriately cinematic, while others appear almost entirely out of focus. Inspect the underlying causes, including depth of field, motion blur, temporal antialiasing, bloom, render resolution and post-processing.

* Maintain sharp, clearly defined characters.
* Use physically believable depth of field.
* Ensure camera focus follows the subject.
* Reserve stronger motion blur for genuinely fast movements.
* Avoid full-screen softness.
* Preserve environmental textures and architectural details.
* Prevent bright lighting and effects from washing out the image.
* Do not compensate with excessive artificial sharpening.

The goal is clean, crisp, stylized 3D animation with selective cinematic effects.
