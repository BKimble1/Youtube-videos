# Website trailer V2: direction (as given by the creator)

Create V2 of the official Ultimate Trifecta website trailer using the existing V1 as a foundation.

IMPORTANT: This is the official promotional trailer for Ultimate Trifecta as a game, NOT the Season 1 or Christmas cinematic trailer. These are two separate projects.

The objective is to get someone who has never played Ultimate Trifecta excited enough to download it. It should feel like a professionally edited trailer from an established game studio.

## Creative direction

Reference the production quality, camera direction, character personality, humor, and musical synchronization of Fortnite's official promotional trailers, while maintaining Ultimate Trifecta's own visual identity.

Make the world feel alive, competitive, playful, social, and genuinely fun to explore.

## Suggested sequence

* 0–3 seconds: Immediately hook the viewer with an exciting, visually impressive moment.
* 3–7 seconds: Reveal the campus and establish the world.
* 7–14 seconds: Showcase entertaining character interactions and distinct gameplay mechanics.
* 14–22 seconds: Increase the energy with competition, abilities, vehicles, customization, and other exciting features that actually exist in the game.
* 22–27 seconds: Deliver a memorable, humorous or exciting climactic sequence.
* 27–30 seconds: Finish with a polished Ultimate Trifecta logo reveal.

Treat this sequence as creative guidance, not a rigid shot list.

## Improvements over V1

* Significantly reduce repetitive running sequences.
* Replace excessive motion-blur transitions with intentional cinematic cuts and motivated camera movements.
* Increase the variety of environments, camera angles, actions, and character interactions.
* Improve character animations, expressions, foot placement, physics, and environmental interactions.
* Use foreground objects, depth, lighting, shadows, and composition to give shots more cinematic quality.
* Make each shot feel deliberately connected to the previous or following shot.
* Retain the strongest moments from V1, including the fountain splash, portal, vehicle, and group character scene where appropriate.
* Synchronize major actions, transitions, and the final logo reveal with the music.
* Incorporate subtle, high-quality sound effects that give actions physical weight.

## Strict requirements

* Use authentic game assets, environments, characters, and implemented mechanics.
* Do not misrepresent the game.
* No narration.
* No generic promotional text scattered throughout the trailer.
* No excessive visual effects.
* No AI-looking graphics.
* No Season 1 subtitle on the final logo.
* Christmas decorations can remain as environmental details, but Christmas should not be the central premise.

Review the game repository and available assets before making creative decisions. Prioritize quality over quantity. Review the entire rendered trailer for animation glitches, awkward transitions, pacing issues, clipping, and visual inconsistencies before delivering it.

Produce a high-quality 4K master and a compressed 1080p website-ready version optimized for fast loading.

Do not simply polish V1. Make a meaningful improvement in its direction, entertainment value, and production quality.
