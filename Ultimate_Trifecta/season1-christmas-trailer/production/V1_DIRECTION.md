# Creator's direction (verbatim), 17 Nov 2026

Ultimate Trifecta | Season 1 Christmas Trailer | V1
Create an absolutely incredible 30-second cinematic launch trailer for the Ultimate Trifecta Season 1 Christmas update.
Eventually, this trailer will automatically play in full screen the first time a player opens the app after installing the Season 1 update. However, for now, focus exclusively on producing the trailer. Do not integrate it into the app yet.
Research and preparation
Before beginning, study professional Fortnite cinematic and seasonal launch trailers, particularly:

* Fortnite Winterfest 2024: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPRvP3-1k2Q
* Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 2: LAWLESS
* Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 1: HUNTERS
* Other high-energy Fortnite seasonal reveal trailers.

Study their camera movements, transitions, pacing, music synchronization, sound design, comedic timing and reveal sequences. Take inspiration from their production quality, not their intellectual property.
Thoroughly inspect the Ultimate Trifecta repository, existing game environment, characters, animations, cosmetics, Christmas decorations, lighting, logos and gameplay mechanics.
Use our Future Got Weird video production workflow where applicable, including Remotion, programmatic animation, custom camera movements, rendering and iterative quality control. However, this must feel like a cinematic 3D gaming trailer, not an animated explainer.
Creative direction
The trailer should make players genuinely excited to play the new season.
I want energy, action, personality, suspense and Christmas atmosphere. Make it feel like a big-budget seasonal gaming event.
Use actual Ultimate Trifecta assets and environments. The trailer should be immediately recognizable as our game.
Trailer sequence
0:00–0:04 | The opening
Open on the Christmas-decorated campus at night. Snow is falling, Christmas lights are glowing, and the camera moves rapidly through the environment.
A police flashlight suddenly swings toward the camera. A character turns around.
The music begins building.
0:04–0:10 | Christmas has arrived
The music drops into an extremely catchy, high-energy Christmas action soundtrack.
Reveal the Christmas environment, seasonal outfits, rewards and new content through dynamic camera movements and fast, creative transitions.
Make everything feel alive.
0:10–0:18 | Absolute chaos
Rapidly transition between exciting gameplay moments, characters running, narrow escapes, police encounters, seasonal cosmetics and competition.
Synchronize major actions and camera cuts with the music.
Build momentum rather than making this feel like disconnected gameplay recordings.
0:18–0:28 | The final chase
Transition seamlessly into one continuous cinematic chase sequence.
Follow a player sprinting through campus at night. In the distance behind him, a police officer appears to be chasing him.
A bright green directional arrow path on the ground guides the player toward the water and eventually back to the dorm.
Use dynamic third-person camera movements, low-angle tracking shots and cinematic perspectives while maintaining spatial continuity.
The player runs into the water, creating a satisfying splash, then continues toward the dorm.
The officer should appear to be genuinely chasing the player, but secretly be letting him win. He should remain far enough behind that the player can escape, without making the chase feel staged.
As the runner approaches the dorm, the music reaches its biggest and most satisfying climax.
Make this the most memorable part of the entire trailer.
0:28–0:30 | Season reveal
Transition into a powerful reveal of the official Ultimate Trifecta logo and the Season 1 Christmas branding.
Use the actual UT logo, not the app icon.
Finish with a memorable musical sting.
Music and sound design
Sound design is just as important as the visuals.
Create or source an original, commercially usable, extremely catchy Christmas action soundtrack.
Incorporate:

* Heavy cinematic percussion
* Sleigh bells
* Punchy bass
* Festive orchestral melodies
* Cinematic risers and impacts
* Running footsteps
* Police radio chatter
* Distant sirens
* Water splashes
* Environmental sounds
* Satisfying transition sounds
* A powerful final logo impact

Layer sound effects carefully. Every major action should have an appropriate sound. Use moments of silence and anticipation to make the musical drops even more impactful.
No narration.
Quality requirements

* Extremely engaging pacing.
* Professional cinematic camera choreography.
* Beautiful nighttime lighting and shadows.
* Smooth, believable character animations.
* Consistent character appearance.
* Creative, seamless transitions.
* No clipping, floating characters or animation glitches.
* No unnecessary text.
* No em dashes.
* No generic AI graphics or circled text.
* No copyrighted Fortnite assets or music.
* No advertising features that do not exist in the game.

Take creative liberties where they improve the trailer. Do not interpret this storyboard so literally that it limits your creativity.
Quality over quantity. I would rather have 30 incredible seconds than 30 seconds of mediocre footage.
